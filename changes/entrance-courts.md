# Entrance courts lead down to the city below

- Every entrance to the city under the land is now a built court: a round sunken hollow lined with a stone wall, a paved floor and a stone walk along its rim.
- Two stairs curve down along the wall from a landing at the rim's lowest point. Opposite them, where the wall is highest, a stone portal frames tall bronze lift doors, with lamps on either side. Stone benches stand at the wall's foot.
- Each district builds its courts its own way. Orchards use warm sandstone, water districts pale limestone and makers glazed brick. Where the land was let go, the courts have no railings and the stone is weathered.
- Courts show from across the cylinder and gain stairs, railings, doors and benches as you come closer. You can walk on the floor, the stairs and the walk, and plants keep out of the courts. The debug menu's Go group takes you into a court, onto its rim walk or over it.
