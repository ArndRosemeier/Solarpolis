# The bore is a hall, not a pipe

- The tube you float through after docking now has hoops of warm light every two radii, each throwing a pool of light on the walls, so the hall reads in rhythm as you drift down it.
- Its walls are warm ivory panels with dark seams between them; the hoops' frames are teal in the still part and terracotta in the turning sleeve, so you see which side of the bearing you are on.
- Four guide rails run its length on stand-offs, each with a brass handhold beside it every few metres.
- Amber chevrons on the walls point the way to the Hub.
- At the bearing, bronze races meet at the seal: the still side carries a whole ring of light, the sleeve a broken ring that turns with the habitat, so you see the spin begin.
