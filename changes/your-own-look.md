# Make your own character

- Your link has a new offer, "Change how you look". It holds up a mirror: the camera turns round to face you, and Actors' new character creator opens beside it. Drag with the right button to walk round yourself; the wheel brings you close enough to see your face.
- Be a human or a robot, and wear anything Actors has: today's clothes, the Commonwear of 3026, medieval cuts, the fantastic. Each part has a Simple tab with presets and a Detailed one with every slider.
- Your look is kept for your next visit. Start over gives back the person the world first made you.
- Residents still dress as people of 3026 do, and are all humans or robot shells.
