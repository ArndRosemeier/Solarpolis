# The Threshold: galleries down the Gate face

- The arrival terrace opens sideways into a long timber-decked gallery, and two more step down the face in front of it, joined by broad low-gravity steps you bound down: a cliff monastery at the habitat's front door.
- The port door now stands in a dressed-stone portal with warm light through a fanlight over it.
- Warm stone and timber instead of grey slabs, benches along the galleries, and a long bronze arrow on the upper gallery pointing the way the habitat spins.
- Lanterns glow on the galleries and all the way down the Lift Stair, and bronze lines cross its steps where gravity reaches a twentieth, a tenth, a fifth and half of the floor's.
- The capsule berth moves down to the lowest gallery.
