# The arrival from inside the ship

- Halfway through the approach the film cuts inside: you stand in the lounge under the ship's nose, behind the couches, looking out of the tall window that faces the port as the ship creeps in along its berth arm.
- The lounge is the ship's own deck behind the panoramic windows you see from outside, with its couches facing the glass, a core up the middle and lamps in a cove round the ceiling.
- It is lit by its lamps, by the bright view out of the windows and by the Sun where it shines in through the glass, and your eyes adjust to the room and the view together.
