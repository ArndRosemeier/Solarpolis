# Seam index

Each row is a question you will have while coding, and the one place that answers it. Before writing a helper, look here. A new seam gets its row in the same PR. *Planned* rows name the seam to build when it is needed.

## The habitat blueprint (`src/blueprint`, pure)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| What are the inputs that define a habitat? | `HabitatParams` (`params.ts`). Add a field there, never a constant elsewhere. |
| What follows from them (spin, hull, light, heat, population...)? | `derive(params)` → `Habitat` (`derive.ts`). |
| How thick are the pillars that carry the land over the living layer? | `habitat.pillars` (`derive.ts`): radius, load and gravity of one pillar of the Understorey from `HabitatParams.under` (bay, stone, land), sized against crushing and buckling; `validate` flags pillars too thick for their bay. |
| How strong is gravity, or how dense is the air, at a radius? | `habitat.gravityAt(r)`, `habitat.pressureAt(r)`. |
| How far does a dropped thing drift (Coriolis)? | `habitat.dropOffset(h)`. |
| What shape is the inside (floor, endcap slopes)? | `habitat.profile` (`makeProfile`, `profile.ts`): the meridian curve, sampled by arc length `s`. |
| Where is a point in the world, and back? | `toWorld(profile, {theta, s, h})`, `fromWorld(profile, x, y, z)`, `groundNormal` (`frame.ts`). World axis is +z from the wild wall (z = 0) to the port wall (z = L); spin is +θ. |
| What does the habitat look like from outside (endcap faces, radiator brims, dock spindle, mast)? | `habitat.exterior` (`makeExterior`, `exterior.ts`), sized from the heat in `derive`; shape inputs in `HabitatParams.exterior`. |
| How far is a point from the habitat's outside? | `exteriorDistance(habitat.exterior, p)` (`exterior.ts`): the nearest of `exterior.pieces` (drum, brims, spindle, mast), negative inside. |
| How much of the skin pipes light in, and how much makes power? | `habitat.collectorShare` (`derive.ts`): light collectors sized to the piped light (`pipedLight`, `collectorEfficiency`, `SUNLIGHT_VISIBLE`); the rest of the skin is solar cells (`solarPower`). |
| Is this habitat buildable, and what is odd about it? | `validate(habitat)` → findings `invalid` / `unusual`; `isValid` (`validate.ts`). |
| How do a habitat's people like to shape their land? | Its culture: `CULTURES` / `cultureOf(name)` (`cultures.ts`), named by `HabitatParams.culture` (an unknown name is an invalid habitat): the district styles (`DISTRICT_STYLES`, `StyleSpec` with the care each is at home in), and what each style makes of its land in any climate (`STYLE_LAND`: crown cover, patch grain, wetness, how strongly it holds to them; read by `gen/ecology.ts`). |
| Which habitats do tests run on? | `MATRIX` and `REFERENCE`, `withParams` (`reference.ts`). One terrain and ecology per habitat for the whole run: `terrainOf(name, params?)` / `ecologyOf` (`test/worlds.ts`); a test of being the same every time builds its own. |

## Generating the habitat (`src/gen`, pure)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| What did the habitat's mind plan for its land (care, gravity bands, valleys, the Gate's faces, the Mountain, river roles, districts and their styles, entrances, landmark sites, the Sunwalk and the ways)? | `planOf(habitat)` (`gen/plan.ts`; made once per habitat by `makePlan`, then kept), the macro layout stage that every later stage reads. Its big shapes, which the land itself is made from, are `landformOf(habitat)` (`Landform`, kept; cheap, no districts): `careAt(s)`, `gravityAt(s)` / `bandAt(s)` (`GRAVITY_BANDS`), `valleyAt(k, s)` / `acrossAt(θ, s)` / `ridgeAt(θ, s)` (the valleys spiral up the endcaps by `plan.spiral`), `faces` / `faceAt(θ, s)`, `mountain`, `rivers` (`PlannedRiver`: role, valley, spring; `routeOf(river, s)`), `endcapAt(wild, g)`, `stairHalfWidth`; the plan adds `districts` / `districtAt(θ, s)` (borders bend with the land) / `borderAt(θ, s)` (the district, how far inside it, the one across the nearest border), `sites` (`Site`: kind, θ, s, size, facing, district; landmarks first, then entrances, each over a bay's middle below, `baysOf`), `paths` (`sunwalk`, `way`, `trail`). The ring lake is the plan's: `lakeOf(habitat)`, which the terrain uses; `fromLake(lake, s)` how far from its shore. Sizes from `HabitatParams.plan`, styles from the culture (`CULTURES` / `cultureOf` in `blueprint/cultures.ts`). Drawn as maps in the Blueprint tab (`lab/planMap.ts`: `floorMap`, `endcapMap`). |
| What lies under the land, bay by bay (wellheads, halls, homes, lanes, galleries, cisterns, the Dim)? | `underOf(habitat)` (`gen/under.ts`, kept): the Understorey's grid of bays (`baysOf`) with `useAt(i, j)` (worked out per bay, nothing stored) and `hamletOf`; `hamlets` in the plan's order of entrances (the courts residents live under, src/sim), each with its rings in bays; `sharesOf` counts the uses. How high it lies is the terrain's: `levelsOf`. Maps in the Blueprint tab (`src/lab/underMap.ts`, on the panels' shared `MapsPanel` in `src/lab/planView.ts`). |
| How high does the Understorey lie under the land, and where is its floor? | `levelsOf(terrain)` (`gen/underLevels.ts`, kept): the layer follows the land (one height, `layers.living`): at each pillar (`at(i, j)`) its ceiling lies under the lowest ground of the four bays round it by the thinnest soil and the deck, no steeper than `under.grade` toward any lower one near (pillars within a few bays, then coarse samples of blocks further off); `levelAt(θ, s)` runs straight between a bay's pillars (`Levels`: ceiling, springing, floor). `underFloorAt` (`gen/underMesh.ts`) is what one stands on below. |
| How is the Understorey built and drawn (pillars, fan vaults, floors, lamps)? | `gen/underMesh.ts`: a `detailTree` grid over its bays (`underGridOf`), tiles wanted only while the eye is below the land (`isBelow`, `underTilesFor`), `buildUnderTile` (request kind `'tiled'`, part `'under'`; far, mid, near by tile size, the same sizes and light) with the lamps' light and the day's down the light wells baked per vertex (`glow`); `underSolid` for the physics' patch below. Through the vault at a bay's middle: under each court its light well (up to the court's glass, `lightWellOf` in gen/courts.ts), and in the next bay its portal faces its lift's open bronze cage (`underLiftOf(terrain, court)`: bay, the way toward the court, side, the height its doors open at above; `underLiftIn(terrain, i, j)` the lift whose cage stands in a bay). On screen a `TiledView` (`underSource`, `inside`: no far form of its own) of the VAULT kind; `follow(eye, want)` asks for the tiles around another point the eye will soon be at (a lift's floor below), and `complete` says whether all it wants are built. |
| What sky does a hamlet show on its ceilings? | `gen/skies.ts` (pure): `SKIES` the catalogue, `hamletSkiesOf(habitat)` each hamlet's pick by its district's style (seeded, kept), `skyOverBay(habitat, i, j)` the sky over a bay (−1: the wellhead, which keeps its stone round the real light, and every bay outside a hamlet). The bay's spandrel carries it as finish `FINISH.screen + k` (gen/underMesh.ts); the VAULT kind draws it with `hamletSky(dir, up, k, day, clock)` (render/hamletSkies.ts: seen infinitely far off, by direction in the eye's frame; `SKY_DRAWN` in the catalogue's order). A new sky is a catalogue entry, its style weights and one branch there. Debug menu `Below: under the next kind of hamlet sky`; shots view `under-skies`. |
| How does one ride a lift between the land and the Understorey? | `gen/lifts.ts` (pure): a lift's doors (`doorsAbove` the court's portal, `doorsBelow` its cage; `liftDoorsNear(terrain, eye, below)` the ones in reach, `doorsHit(doors, eye, dir)` whether a click's ray meets them), the cabin's frame at a share of its ride (`cabinPlace`, `rideOf`), the ride's pace (`rideSeconds`, `rideShare`, `calledFrom`), where one steps out (`outside`), and the cabin itself (`buildCabin`). On screen `CabinView` (render/lift.ts): the one cabin drawn, the last used lift's, and its ride; InteriorView boards, waits for the floor below, rides and steps out. Debug menu: `To a lift: at its doors…`, `Below: at a lift cage…`; shots view `lift-ride`. |
| How do I file things by where they lie on the floor, to find the ones near a point? | `floorGrid(radius, cell)` (`gen/floorGrid.ts`): columns around (wrapping, at least `cell` wide) and rows along, `key(column, row)`, `column(θ)`, `row(s)`; `fileUnder(map, key, item)`. Used by the plan's entrances, the paths' pieces and the places' blocks. |
| Where are the Understorey's bays (its pillar grid), and which bay is a point over? | `baysOf(habitat)` (`gen/bays.ts`, kept): the floor's grid (`floorGrid`) at `under.bay`, rows centred on the flat floor: `around`, `along`, `dTheta`, `bay`, `s0`, `key(i, j)`, `bayAt(θ, s)`, `centreOf(i, j)`, `middleNear(θ, s, sMin?, sMax?)` (a bay's middle near a point: the plan sets every entrance there, so a court's light well comes down where the vault reaches the deck). `underOf` is built on it (`Under extends Bays`). |
| How high is the ground at (θ, s)? | `makeTerrain(habitat)` (`terrain.ts`): `heightAt`, `standAt` (ground or water surface), `waterDepth(p)`, `lake`; sizes from `HabitatParams.relief`. Noise is sampled at world positions, so it wraps. The floor follows the plan's valleys (`FLOOR_VALLEYS`: it rises from each valley's middle to the ridges between, with fewer hills on the valley floors). Each entrance digs its court (`courtOf(h, site)` / `COURT` in gen/plan.ts): `terrain.courts` (with each floor's height, its depth under the lowest rim) and `terrain.courtAt(θ, s)`; the walls, stairs and floor are the landmarks'. The endcaps are the plan's landforms (`terrain.form`, sizes from `relief.massif`): the Mountain's couloirs, spurs, crags, horn (`HORN`) and cirque (`CIRQUE`) with natural benches; the Gate's faces between buttresses, terraced by kind (`GATE_TERRACES`), the Lift Stair's ramp up the Stair face. |
| How steep is the ground at a point? | `groundSlope(n, θ)` (`gen/terrain.ts`): 0 level … 1 a wall, against true up (toward the axis), from the ground's normal; `slopeAt(ecology, θ, s, ground, step)` (`gen/scatter.ts`) finds the normal from the heights around. Tiles, plants and the meter all use it. |
| Where do the paths run in the land, how wide are they, what are they laid with? | `pathsOf(habitat)` (`gen/paths.ts`, kept): the plan's `paths` (`PlanPath`: kind, `width` from `HabitatParams.plan.pathWidth`; the ways swing gently between entrances) as straight pieces in a grid; `pathAt(θ, s, reach?)` the nearest within its shoulders (or `reach` m of its edge): kind, width, distance from the middle line, the middle point, `layer` (the Sunwalk gravel-pale stone, a way its district's `STYLE_LAND[style].way`, the trail dirt), the piece and how far along. `makeTerrain` levels the ground across each (`gradePath`: `CROWN`, `SHOULDER`; the level taken every `LEVEL_STEP` widths along over the carved land; not in the lake or a river's water); `colorAt` lays the surface over the share of its patch a path covers (`pathCover`, its `cell` argument, so a far path is a faint line), and keeps crowns off the Sunwalk and the ways; scatter keeps plants and stones off. `nearestOnPath` for the debug Go entries. |
| Where do the rivers run, and how deep is the water at a point? | `makeRivers` (`gen/rivers.ts`), laid over the land by `makeTerrain`, which carves their channels and valleys into `heightAt`; `terrain.rivers`, `terrain.riverAt(θ, s)` (in the water or null). A `River` is samples along s (middle θ unwrapped, surface, half width, run, steepness); `riverAt(rivers, θ, s, reach)` the nearest within a reach (banks, valleys), `riverPoint(river, s)` between samples, `nearestSample` / `besideRiver` for the debug Go entries. Each follows its `PlannedRiver` (role, spring, `routeOf`; spiralling up the endcaps, meandering only on the floor), mouths in the ring lake (no lake, no rivers); sizes from `HabitatParams.relief.rivers`. |
| How is the water drawn (the lake, the rivers)? | `buildWater(terrain, tile, origin)` (`gen/water.ts`), called by `buildTile`: the lake's grid and each river's ribbon clipped to the tile, one mesh per tile with `flow` (across, run, speed, steepness) and `dir` (downstream, half width); on tiles too coarse for a channel, the ribbon lies over the ground the tile draws (`WaterTile.drawn`), so a far river never sinks. Shaded by WATER (`materials.ts`): ripples carried by the flow, white water by steepness, the sunline's glint. |
| What grows where, and what colour is the ground? | `makeEcology(terrain)` (`gen/ecology.ts`): `biomesAt(θ, s, h, slope, out)` (shares of the climate's biomes; on the worked floor each district's style leans on them by `STYLE_LAND`, and belts of the closest woods run along the district borders, from `plan.borderAt`), `moistureAt`, `colorAt` (biomes, then gravel by rivers, sand, the lake's and rivers' beds, and rock; with a `canopy` array also the woods' canopy layer: the crowns' colour and height, and with a `wood` array their cover and spacing, from `canopyOf(biome)`), `nearestWood` / `nearestFloor` (debug Go entries: the nearest closed wood, the nearest meadow). With a `floor` array, each point's shares of the `FLOOR_LAYERS` (grass, litter, sand, rock …: 0–255, 8 per point), from each biome's `floor` and the same overlays; tiles carry them as `floorA`/`floorB`. The biomes and where each is planted come from the habitat's climate: `CLIMATES` / `climateOf(name)` in `blueprint/climates.ts`, named by `HabitatParams.climate` (an unknown name is an invalid habitat). Sizes are shares of the radius. |
| What plants are there, and what do they look like? | Each biome's `plants` (`PlantSpec` in `blueprint/climates.ts`: one of `PLANT_FORMS`, height and spacing in metres). `buildTree(form, seed, variant, detail)` (`gen/trees.ts`, ported from Scale): one unit high, wood (bark-atlas uv), leaf cards (leaf-atlas uv, tint, occlusion), crown hull; `LITE_DETAIL` for the lighter tree. Textures: `barkAtlas()` (`gen/foliage.ts`) and `leafAtlas()` (`workers/leaves.ts`, OffscreenCanvas), both drawn in a worker (`kind: 'foliage'`); shapes too (`kind: 'tree'`). |
| Where does each plant stand, and at which level is it drawn? | `gen/scatter.ts`: each plant's own lattice (any cell size gives the same plants). `LEVELS` (full, lite, hull) with their fade bands in sight units (`unit(spec)`); `plantsOf(ecology)` (species with per-level cell sizes and bands in metres), `canopyBand(ecology)` (where the ground's canopy takes over the woods), `cellsNear(ecology, species, level, eye, from?)` (each with its `near` and `far`; `from` 0 for a level that may stand in for a finer one), `plantCell(ecology, plants, key)` (instances per shape, `PLANT_STRIDE` floats each), `cellBytes`. Where a species' plants stand within bounds, without making a cell: `standsIn(ecology, species, bounds, level, visit)`, the one walk of the lattice (`plantCell` uses it). Runs in the generation workers (`kind: 'plants'`). Ground cover (the climate's `cover`, `CoverSpec`: grass on its floor layer) is a species too (`biome` −1, `on` its layer): drawn at the full and lite levels only (`COVER_FADE`, metres), planted with the chance `coverChance(ecology, layer, grid)` (the layer's share on a lattice, blended; `gridOf(species, level)`). Debris (the climate's `debris`, `DebrisSpec`: stones, fallen trunks and branches) the same way but at all three levels, seen by its size (`debrisUnit`), each carrying the ground's slope under it (`PLANT_STRIDE` 7: x, y, z, height, turn, tilt around and along; the kinds shear by it in `plantFrame`). `nearestOf(ecology, plants, species, eye)` for the debug Go entries. |
| What do stones and fallen wood look like? | `buildStone(color, moss, seed, variant)` (`gen/stones.ts`): one lump (ellipsoid, breaks, noise) sampled at three icosphere levels (`icosphere(n)` in `gen/shapes.ts`, the one sphere), vertex colours; the STONE kind adds a grain in metres. `buildFallenWood(form, 'log' or 'branch', seed, variant)` (`gen/trees.ts`): the form's bark on bent tubes, stubs or side limbs, cut ends; drawn with BARK. Workers: `kind: 'stone'`, `kind: 'fallen'`. |
| What does grass look like up close? | `buildTuft(form, color, seed, variant)` (`gen/grass.ts`): a full and a light tuft of blades, one unit high, vertex colours and sway weights; built in a worker (`kind: 'tuft'`), drawn by `PlantView` with the GRASS kind (waves of wind along the axis; the last level sinks tufts into the floor, `userData.sink`). Past 40 m the floor's grass texture is the cover. |
| What hides the sunline from a ground point? | `horizonAt(terrain, θ, s)` (`horizon.ts`): the horizon angles toward the port and the wild end, from the ground in the point's meridian; tiles bake them per point (`TileMesh.horizon`). |
| How is the ground cut into pieces for drawing, and which pieces does a view need? | The tile tree (`tiles.ts`): `makeLayout` (bands along s, columns by circumference), `roots`, `children(layout, k)`, `parent(layout, k)`: a tile halves along s always, around the axis only while its row stays wide enough (`splitsAround`, `splitAround`), so tiles stay about square where the endcaps' rings shrink to the axis. `select(terrain, layout, eye, pixelsPerRadian, maxPixels, fitOf)` by `splitError(layout, k, error)`: the measured error on screen, but never past cells of `FINEST_CELL_PIXELS` (rock walls never get more accurate, so their error alone would split every tile that sees one to the finest level), and cells no bigger than `CELL_PIXELS` (so far tiles carry the woods' edges, rivers and relief), at `ERROR_PIXELS`; a `maxPixels` above it draws everything coarser, each built tile's distance from its own `relief` (`TileFit`): `draw`, `want`, and `keep` (built tiles in use besides those drawn: the ones split to reach them, children waiting for a sibling; the cache must never evict them). `buildTile` (mesh relative to its `origin`, skirts, water (`buildWater`), `morph` towards the parent's surface). |
| What is each panel of the outer skin (solar cell, light collector, starfloor window), how old, how does it look? | `makeSkin(habitat)` (`skin.ts`): `panelAt(skin, i, j)`, `panelOf(skin, p)`, `lookOf`, `isCollector`, `campaignAt`, from integer hashes of the skin's seed; the shader (`render/hullShaders.ts`) mirrors the same hashes bit for bit, so the interior's starfloor rooms read the windows here. `columnsOf` and `skinFlux` give a far habitat's light (its glint). |
| Where are the maintenance drones and their tracks? | `isTrack(skin, i)`, `tracksOf`, `dronesAt(skin, t)` (`skin.ts`): a track in the seam below every `exterior.drones.trackEvery`-th column, each drone back and forth over its own stretch, from seed and clock. `trackMean` is how the far view counts them. |
| What ground does the physics stand on? | `buildPatch(terrain, θ, s, size, cell)` (`patch.ts`): a square trimesh of the ground around a point, relative to its `origin`; built in the worker pool (request kind `'patch'`). It carries the landmarks' walkable solids too (the pavilion's `'solid'` level, near the patch, the Lift Stair's `buildStairSolid`, and the solids of every kind of thing at sites (`courtSites`, `crossingSites`, `hallSites`: `.solids`)), each cut to the patch by `keepNear`; asked `below`, its middle is on the Understorey's floor and it holds `underSolid` too (`GroundPatch.below`). |
| What does the skin look like up close (bays, modules, beams, bolts, cables)? | `gen/hullDetail.ts`: each skin panel is a bay of `Skin.modules`² solar modules (`exterior.parts`: module and cell size); `skinGrid`/`skinEye` lay it out for the detail tree (`wantedTiles(skin, eye)`), `buildHullTile(skin, key)` one tile's glass (with its `grid` attribute) and parts, more of them the smaller the tile (`detailOf`, sizes in `PART` as shares of bay, module or track). Each tile owns its parts exactly (`cellsOf` cuts: areas cut, bars by their middle line, fittings by their centre), so any two levels meet. Built in the worker pool as the first part of `detailParts` (request kind `'hull'`, part `'skin'`). `railTop` is where the drones ride. |
| How does detail come in as the eye comes closer (any surface or part)? | `gen/detailTree.ts` (pure): a part lays its cells out as a `Grid` (around, wrapping or not, and along; root and finest levels); `wantedCells(grid, eye)` is the quadtree around a `GridEye` (split within `SPLIT` × a tile's edge, roots within `reachOf(grid)`), `cellsOf(grid, key)` the cells a tile holds with what it owns of each (`CellCut`), `coverOf(wanted, built)` what to draw while tiles stream in (a parent until all its children are built) and whether a part is wholly covered, `rootTiles(grid)` the far form. A built thing that streams in tiles (gen side: a grid, tiles for an eye, a tile builder and a worker request kind) is drawn by `TiledView` (`render/tiled.ts`) from a `TiledSource` (`stairSource`; `siteSource` for any kind of thing at sites: `courtSource`, `crossingSource`, `hallSource`; `underSource`, seen only from inside, so `inside`: only the tiles the eye wants): roots always kept, finer tiles replace them only once built. Every detailed part uses it; never a quadtree of its own. |
| What does the hardware look like up close (spindle plating, berth arms, mast, dishes)? | `gen/hullParts.ts` (pure): `detailParts(habitat, skin)` lists every detailed part with its frame (`'body'` or the despun `'spindle'`), its `Grid` and its `Place`; `partEye`/`partDistance`/`partTiles` place an eye over it, `buildPartTile(habitat, skin, part, key)` builds one tile (the skin's through `buildHullTile`), more parts the smaller the tile (`partDetail`, sizes in `PLATING`, `TRUSS`, `DISH` as shares of the plate, truss width or dish diameter, `exterior.parts.handrail` for what people hold). A truss's or dish's root tile (`wholeTile`) is the whole part and is its far form too. `antennas(habitat)` places the dishes and the mast's whips; `berthArmAt(spindle, i)` (blueprint/exterior.ts) is which way an arm points, for the arms, their collars and the ships berthed there. |
| Where do the landmarks stand, and how far do they keep plants off? | `landmarksOf(terrain)` (`gen/landmarks.ts`), each on its site in the habitat plan (`siteOf(planOf(habitat), kind)`, gen/plan.ts), sized by `HabitatParams.landmarks`: the pavilion on the `lake-pavilion` site (where the Sunwalk crosses the lake), a bridge to each shore along the axis; the summit hut on the `summit-hut` site, hung on the Mountain's cliff (its hall clear of the rock, its anchor and struts buried in it). `frameOf(habitat, landmark)` is its `Place` (z up; the pavilion's x along the axis, the hut's out toward the view; `placeAt(habitat, θ, s, height)` in gen/shapes.ts for any spot); `buildLandmark(landmark, level)` builds either kind at a level (`LANDMARK_LEVELS`, gen/pavilion.ts), `sightOf` is how the view picks the level; `keptClear` (scatter skips it), `landmarkTopAt` (what people stand on; the view's `standOn`), `reachOf`. A new landmark takes its plan site here. |
| How does the Lift Stair climb the Gate, and what does a tile of it hold? | `liftStairOf(habitat)` (`gen/liftStair.ts`, kept; null without a ramp wide enough): platforms and legs in plan over `rampOf(habitat)` (z along the axis, w across the ramp, e height above the floor; `world`/`plan` map to and from the world), risers by gravity (`riserAt`), zigzag where straight flights would be gentler than the face. `legShapesOf(stair)` lays each leg out (steps, aprons, the nosing line `lineAt`); `stairTopAt` is its walkable top in plan, `stairStandAt` / `stairKeepsClear` the same over the land (through `landmarkTopAt` / `keptClear`). Its tiles are a `detailTree` grid over the ramp: `stairGridOf`, `stairTilesFor(habitat, eye)`, `buildStairTile` (request kind `'stair'`; far, mid and near detail by tile size), `buildStairSolid` (in every ground patch); on screen a `TiledView` (`render/tiled.ts`, `stairSource`). |
| How is an entrance court built, and what does one stand on in it? | `courtLayoutOf(terrain, index)` (`gen/courts.ts`, laid out once per court over the terrain's `courts`, which dig them): rim samples, a landing at the lowest rim flush with the walk, two stairs down along the wall one each way (one, steeper, where two would not fit; risers by `riserAt`), the portal with bronze lift doors facing one of the four ways along the bays below (its lift comes down in the next bay that way), where the wall is highest clear of them, benches; `lightWellOf(habitat, court)` its light well's glass. Stone and finish by district style (`STYLE_STONE`; let-go courts unrailed). `buildCourt(layout, detail)` in the court's floor frame (x along, y around, z up from its dug floor; `courtWorld`); its tiles are site tiles, `courtSites` (`gen/siteTiles.ts`; request kind `'courts'`). `courtSites.topAt` is what one stands on (through `landmarkTopAt`); `keptClear` keeps plants off a court and its clear ring (`terrain.courtAt`). On screen a `TiledView` (`courtSource`). |
| How is a thing built on the floor at a site streamed in tiles, and bent onto the curve? | A thing is built flat in its `FloorFrame` (`gen/shapes.ts`: θ, s, its height over the profile and a heading from +s toward +θ; x along the heading, y to its left, z up): `floorAt` / `inFloorFrame` map frame and floor, `floorPoint` a frame point into the world, `bendInto(target, h, frame, shape, place)` bends a built shape onto the floor into a tile's place (normals turned with it), `solidOnFloor` its solid level into the world for the physics, `topOnFloor` turns a top in the frame into a height over the profile. Its tiles: `siteParts(kind)` (`gen/siteTiles.ts`): a `SiteKind` says where its things are (`sites`, `frame`), how one is built at a detail (`build`), and what one stands on (`at` to find it, `top` in its frame); `siteParts` gives back the grid, the things each tile owns, the tiles for an eye, the tile builder (bent onto the floor, a `SiteTile`), the solids for the physics (cached per terrain) and `topAt`. Underneath: `siteGrid`, `sitesIn` (each site in the tile its middle lies in), `siteTilesFor`, `siteTileMiddle`, `siteDetailOf` (far/mid/near by the tile's edge in handrails), `sitesNear`. The entrance courts (`courtSites`), river crossings (`crossingSites`) and grown halls (`hallSites`) use it; a new kind of thing at sites is one `siteParts` call, a worker part in `gen.worker.ts` and a `siteSource` in render/tiled.ts. |

| How is something below a pixel drawn as a point of light? | `pointsGeometry(n)` (`geometry.ts`) with the STARS kind: `setPoint(position, color, i, dir, far, flux)` per point (flux relative to full sunlight), `pointsChanged(mesh)` after. Neighbours' glints, drones and ships use it; never a quad array of their own. |
| How are the drones drawn? | `DroneMesh` (`drones.ts`), one per meshed habitat in `NecklaceView`: a HARDWARE mesh near the skin (`moveTo(t)`), beyond `DRONES_WITHIN` drone lengths one STARS point each (`glow`, with `userData.plainExposure` so they take the eyes' exposure, not the stars'). Nothing vanishes with distance (Arnd, 2026-10-21): each far form carries the near one's light. |
| How does the hull up close (skin, plating, arms, mast, dishes) reach the screen? | `HullDetailView` (`render/hullDetail.ts`), one per habitat the eye is near any detailed part of (`NecklaceView.followDetail`): streams every part's `partTiles` through one `StreamCache`, draws each part's `coverOf` in its frame (`Frames`: the body's or the spindle's), each tile a HARDWARE mesh of its parts and, for the skin, a SKIN_DETAIL mesh of its glass; `covers(part)` tells the necklace when a whole part's far form can hide. Below the smallest tiles the shaders carry on (`skinDetailColor`, `hardwareColor` in `hullShaders.ts`): every fine layer fades in by `shows` (its period on screen), lines are box-filtered by `lineAt`, and `keepMean` keeps each layer's mean, so the near view's light is the far view's. |
| How does the free camera stay with a surface to look at it? | `NecklaceView.holdStation(eye, look, t0, t1)`: near a drum the eye turns with the habitat (fully within `HOLD_NEAR` outer radii, not beyond `HOLD_FAR`), and lets go as the eye comes nearer the despun spindle and its parts than the drum (`SPINDLE_NEAR`, `SPINDLE_FAR`); `OutsideView.frame` applies it each frame. |
| How is a ship drawn? | `ShipMeshes(shape)` (`ship.ts`), the one way a ship enters the scene: `shipGeometry` body as HARDWARE (an albedo per part), a torch's plume as FLAME (additive; `userData.flame` sets length and throttle), `pose(axes, stern, throttle)` with `shipAxes(forward, up)`. Far below a few pixels a ship is a point of its own light (`shipFlux`: sunlit hull plus the plume's glow). The title's ship is placed by `OutsideView` (`startAttract`, `stopAttract`), the traffic's by `TrafficView` (`traffic.ts`, one per habitat drawn as meshes, from `NecklaceView`). |
| How do people cross into the spin? | The bore on the axis, in the blueprint as `exterior.spindle.bore` (radius, the Hub's end, the bearing at the port face, the arms' end); drawn by `BoreMeshes` (`render/bore.ts`, from `boreShapes`) in the home habitat's `NecklaceView` meshes: still walls with the spindle, the sleeve turning with the body, BORE walls lit by their LAMP strips (`boreColor`, `BORE_LIGHT`). Inside it the exposure meter sees only its walls (`Meterable.room`, `NecklaceView.insideBore`). |
| What is drawn inside? | `InteriorView` (`view.ts`): terrain tiles, sunline, the player's body, the walker's or the flyer's camera (`toggleFlying`, key F; on foot `zoom(deltaY)`, the mouse wheel, glides from the eyes out to behind the player). |
| How is a person drawn? | An Actors `Figure` (`render/actors/figure.ts`, ported from Dunfinity): `Figure.load(look, share?)` (`share`: the catalog's loaded human, whose pack, targets and shape worker a resident's figure uses), `update({ feet, up, front, speed, anim }, dt, time)` stands it on the curved floor; `castAsWorn(object)` readies it for the near shadow map; `FigureAnimator` (`animator.ts`) plays the pack's clips per move state, and `anim.pose` a clip (or a held share of one) in place of standing idle. The pack: `HUMAN_PACK`, `packFetch` (a file asked for twice at once is fetched once; nothing is kept once it has arrived), `humanCatalog()` (`pack.ts`; served and built under /actors/ by the `actorsPack` Vite plugin, fetched by `tools/actors/pack.mjs`). Looks: `playerLook(seed)` (`looks.ts`); residents: `Residents.figureLook(resident)` (`residents.ts`). |
| How are the people up on the land drawn around the player? | `PeopleView` (`render/people.ts`, made by `InteriorView`): asks `peopleAt` around the eye every second (or after 100 m), `whereIs` for each person every frame (far ones a share per frame); the nearest few within 30 m get Actors figures (one loading at a time; Actors' levels that draw parts separately, Full and Medium, all compiled before it is shown and picked by distance as Actors does; its merged crowd level is left to the stand-ins), everyone else a stand-in out to where a body is 3 px tall, and beyond, out to 1.5 km, their own speck (SPECK kind, exact place, colours from the dress), handed over in one frame by height on screen with a margin back. People new in the area are placed and measured nearest first, within 3 ms a frame; what is built under each (`landmarkTopAt`) is looked up again only after 20 cm of walking. A figure takes over from its stand-in only in the frame it is first drawn, and gives it back the same way (debug line `people`: handover holes must stay 0). Everyone drawn by these levels is listed in `near` (`personKey`s) each frame, for the far specks to leave out. How someone stands (ground, deck, treading water; upright, kneeling, lying): `footingOf` (`sim/footing.ts`), for every level. |
| What does a resident's stand-in need to match their figure? | `Residents.dressOf(resident)` (`render/actors/residents.ts`): the figure's exact standing height (the base mesh plus the look's weighted morph targets at the soles and crown, without building the body; of each target only its rise there is kept, the target is fetched and let go; checked against Actors' own shapes in `test/slow/residents.slow.test.ts`), its width, and the colours of its skin, hair and garments (a shell's plates) by slot. |
| How is a crowd of stand-ins drawn? | One instanced mesh of the CROWD kind (`materials.ts`): the body from `standInArrays()` (`gen/standIn.ts`: height 1, every part tagged with its colour slot and limb), people put on it with `layInstances(geometry, data, STAND_IN_LAYOUT)` (`STAND_IN_STRIDE` floats each: foot, height, turn, stride, pose, width, horizons, packed colours); `standInPose` (`crowdShaders.ts`) walks, kneels or lays it down. A shape's geometry made instanced: `instancedOf(geometry)` (`geometry.ts`); a mesh drawn wherever it is (not culled by bounds): `drawnAnywhere(geometry, material, name)`. |
| How is someone drawn who covers a few pixels or less? | A speck (SPECK kind, `speckLook` in `crowdShaders.ts`): one quad per person (`quadInstances()`, `geometry.ts`) put on with `layInstances(geometry, data, SPECK_LAYOUT)` (`writeSpeck`, `gen/standIn.ts`: feet in the world, height, way (m/s, carried on by the `speckClock` uniform), pose, three colour bands, horizons, width) and a `speckShown` flag per person; the quad covers the body's box on screen, at least 1.5 px, its opacity the share of it the body fills, so a crowd adds up to its tint (debug menu: `speckMark` shows every speck magenta and at least 4 px, or hides them). Colours: `speckBands(dress.colors)` for a measured look, else `guessBands(look, shell)` from the same dyes and skin tones. |
| How is everyone beyond the people near drawn? | `SpeckView` (`render/specks.ts`, made by `InteriorView`): every 3 s (or when the clock has moved 5 s) the worker job `'specks'` makes everyone up on the land (`specksAt(terrain, t)`, `sim/specks.ts`: sorted by `personKey`, still people's footing kept from the last list); the list goes to the GPU, its CPU copy is let go, and it takes over from the old one in one frame. Each frame the near levels' `near` keys turn their specks off (debug line `specks`). |
| How are the drones drawn? | `FlierView` (`render/fliers.ts`, made by `InteriorView`): every design built in the workers (job `'drone'`, `droneDesign(habitat, role, index)` in `gen/drone.ts`: Actors' kit, `KIT` per role, flattened into the drone's frame with `paint` and `spin` per vertex; the coarse level by `simplify(shape, cell)`, `gen/simplify.ts`), put on the GPU and compiled before any drone shows; then every drone of the fleet each frame at one level by its size on screen: near and coarse as instances of the DRONE and DRONE_VEIL kinds (`flierPose` in `droneShaders.ts`: rotors spin, strobes flash, the body bobs and tilts; `writeFlier`, `FLIER_LAYOUT`), a speck below 4 px (`writeSpeck`, the SPECK kind). All levels written in one frame (the debug line counts `holes`, must stay 0). |
| How are the animals drawn (squirrels, deer)? | `AnimalView` (`render/animals.ts`, one per `Kind`: `SQUIRRELS`, `DEER`, made by `InteriorView`): asks the kind's worker job (`'squirrels'`: `densNear`; `'herds'`: `herdsNear`) for the homes within its range every so often or after a move, gets each home ready nearest first in 2 ms a frame (`ready`/`prepare`: a herd's day laid out, ten minutes ahead too), and where each animal is every frame (`at`); it turns toward its way at the kind's pace. The nearest few big enough on screen get Actors figures (`AnimalFigure`, `render/actors/animal.ts`: the pack's body in its look's preset and size, `paramsOf`, coat baked per look by the worker job `'coats'`, both of Actors' levels that draw parts separately compiled before it is shown, Full and Medium picked by distance as the pack says), everyone else out on the land a speck in its coat's colour bands. A figure takes over from its speck only in the frame it is first drawn (debug lines `squirrels`, `deer`: handover holes must stay 0). An animal the simulation has away (a squirrel up in its tree) is not drawn. A new kind is a `Kind` and a worker job. |
| Which material kit draws Actors characters? | `actorKit` (`render/actors/pack.ts`): Actors' WebGPU `nodeMaterials` (one shader per material kind for every character; face morphs as vertex attributes in one interleaved buffer since Actors 0.6.6, a face peaks at 7 of WebGPU's 8 vertex buffers). Its per-figure textures (eyes, skin photos) need the Stage's `keepTextureUpdates` and `rebindSwitchedTextures` (three r186). People's figures take it as `materials`; animals spread it under their own fur and eyes. |
| How is an Actors animal's fur drawn on WebGPU? | Its coat baked per vertex on the CPU (`bakeCoat(source, coat)` in `gen/coat.ts`, ported from Actors' `fur.ts`: base, saddle, belly, mask, points, stripes and spots, a deer's head tone, dorsal stripe, flank line, rump patch and border, the shells' strand tips averaged in; linear RGB plus the hair's grain), set as the `coat` attribute, drawn by the shared `furMaterial()` (`materials.ts`: the coat with locks of hair from noise along `furFlow`, faded where they would shimmer); eyes by `animalEyeMaterial()`. A deer's spots are smaller than its vertices are apart, so `bakeCoat` gives their pattern per vertex instead (`dapple`: where in the pattern, how strongly, the white) and `furMaterial(true)` draws them per pixel (ragged spots in a staggered grid, Actors' `deerSpots`, fading to their mean where they would shimmer). A preset's shape and coat: `paramsOf(kind, preset)`. `coatsOf(ask, fetcher)` bakes every look of a pack in a worker; `coatBands(coatPos, coat)` its three speck colours. |
| How does a figure loading for someone hand over to it? | `FigureLoads` (`render/actors/slots.ts`): `load(make)` gives a `FigureSlot` the figure arrives in (one loading at a time is the caller's rule, `loading`); `drop(slot)` lets it go, now or when it arrives. The view adds the figure and marks the slot `shown` in the frame it first draws it, and stops drawing its stand-in in that same frame. Used by `PeopleView` and `AnimalView`. |
| How does a view own a layer of specks? | `ownSpecks(name, count)` (`render/specks.ts`): an instanced SPECK mesh with its own data and buffer (`OwnSpecks`), `stage.prepare` it before adding; `roomForSpecks(own, need)` grows it (doubling, same mesh) before writing; `drawSpecks(own, n)` sends and draws the first n. How many pixels a metre covers at 1 m: `pxPerMetre(stage, camera, screen)`. Used by `PeopleView`, `FlierView`, `AnimalView`. |
| How does a figure stand on the curved floor? | `standAt(object, { feet, up, front })` (`render/actors/figure.ts`): feet relative to the eye, up toward the axis, front the way it faces. Used by `Figure` and `AnimalFigure`. |
| How are instances put on a mesh and sent to the GPU? | `layInstances(geometry, data, layout)` (`materials.ts`): `STAND_IN_LAYOUT`, `SPECK_LAYOUT` or `FLIER_LAYOUT`, vec4 attributes from one interleaved buffer (pass another mesh's buffer to share it); `sendInstances(buffer, n, stride)` (`geometry.ts`) sends the first n. Something standing along its up and turned about it: `uprightAt(at, turn)` in a kind (`materials.ts`), its turn from a heading over the floor `turnFor(profile, s, heading)` (`render/people.ts`). |
| What time is it in the habitat? | `InteriorView.time` (clock s since the first dawn), advanced by frame time × `timeScale`; the debug menu's Time group changes it. Nothing reads the wall clock. |
| How does the view run physics? | `InteriorView.simulate(dt, eye)`: asks the pool for a ground patch when the bubble needs one, advances the `PhysicsWorld`, draws each body as a BALL mesh. `dropBall(height)` and `clearBalls()` are in the debug menu's Physics group. |

## The society (`src/sim`, pure)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| Who lives here? | `residentOf(habitat, i)` (`population.ts`): resident i of `HabitatParams.population` is the same person every time (home court, human or mind, grown body or shell, look seed, walking speed). Never shown by the body. |
| Who is up on the land now, and where? | `peopleAt(habitat, t, near?)` (`population.ts`): everyone (or everyone within an area) at clock time t, with position, heading, speed, activity, and the court and outing they came up for. Pure function of seed and clock, no simulation; each court's day is made once and kept (bounded), routes too. Sizes and shares from `HabitatParams.life`. |
| Which person is this, across levels and lists? | `personKey(person)` (`population.ts`): their court and outing as one number, the same for `peopleAt`, `whereIs` and `specksAt`. |
| Where is someone found by `peopleAt` at another moment of the day? | `whereIs(habitat, t, person)` (`population.ts`): by their court and outing, about a microsecond; null once they have gone below. |
| Where is the biggest crowd near a place? | `busiestNear(habitat, t, near, cell?)` (`population.ts`): the middle of the floor square with the most people (the debug menu's "Before the nearest crowd"). |
| Where do people go on the land? | `venuesOf(habitat)` (`venues.ts`): courts (a short stop, `life.pause`), every court's green along its way out (used in its district's style), district commons (activity by style, `STYLE_ACTIVITY`), the Sunwalk near each court, the lake shore, reserve hikes, and the landmarks with their share of all outings (`LANDMARK_SHARE`). Each belongs to the court people come up at to reach it. |
| Who goes up together, and how do they stand? | Parties (`population.ts`, sizes by `life.parties`): neighbouring slots of the day's shuffle (regulars: the same party every week); every `Person` carries `member` and `size` (`outing − member` is the party's first). A party walks two abreast and stands by its place's `Form`: a ring facing in (talk, dance, a workbench), a row (the view, the sky), side by side on the seat round a court's light well (`courtFloorOf` in gen/courts.ts: the seat, the open floor, the walk), or loosely. Who in a party speaks now: `talkerAt(person, t)` (every member agrees; the figures' talk and listen clips in render/people.ts). |
| How do people come up into a court and leave it? | Out of its lift's portal, round the aisle inside its stairs and benches, up the stair nearer the portal, over the landing and out over the walk, then round the rim to where its way leaves: `courtPassageOf(terrain, i)` / `courtWayOut(terrain, i, angle)`; to a spot in the court, round the aisle to its angle (`courtWayIn`) (gen/courts.ts, the court's own layout). The population takes them when given the terrain (`peopleAt(h, t, near, ground)`, `whereIs(h, t, who, ground)`: `wayOf` in population.ts); every level that draws people passes it, so they agree. |
| How do people walk from one place to another? | `walksOf(habitat)` (`walks.ts`): the plan's Sunwalk, ways and trail as one tree; `between(a, b)` is the route along the paths, `nearest` the net's point nearest a place, `pointAlong(route, R, d)` a point on it. |
| Where is a drone, and what is it doing? | `fleetOf(terrain)` (`sim/air.ts`, made once per terrain): every drone's job by role (`HabitatParams.life.drones`, roles `DRONE_ROLES` in the blueprint): couriers on rounds from their court along the ways (`routeOf(habitat, from, to)`, `population.ts`, the one way to a route of the walk net), tenders and others roaming an area, surveyors orbiting, the Gate's lifters shuttling up its face, gliders drifting near the axis. `flierAt(terrain, id, t)`: one drone at clock time t (place, height over the profile, heading, speed, climb, fly/work/rest), a few microseconds; `fliersAt(terrain, t)`: all. |
| Who is a drone, and how is a person in a drone body drawn? | A resident whose `body` is `'drone'` (`residentOf`, share `life.drones.persons`; `design`: which of the `drones.bodies` designs of the `person` role's kit they wear, `designsOf(h, role)` in gen/drone.ts). They live as anyone does (outings, parties, forms, talking turns); `footingOf` hovers them at the eyes of those they are with (over ground, deck or water; never swimming). `PeopleView` finds and follows them and hands them over each frame (`aloft`); `FlierView.follow(eye, t, dt, aloft)` draws them with the fleet at the same levels, nodding on their turn to talk, its buffers growing with how many are near (`room`), and lists them in its `near`. The far specks guess their body from its spec (`designGuess`, sim/specks.ts; `Specks.design`) until the built design's measure replaces it (`Measured` in render/specks.ts, `FlierView.measured(design)`). |
| Where are the squirrels, and what are they doing? | `densNear(ecology, plants, theta, s, radius)` (`sim/fauna.ts`, in the worker): the dens (home trees) within a radius, from cells two ranges a side holding `HabitatParams.life.animals.squirrels` per hectare of closed crowns; each at a tree the scatter really plants (`standsIn`). `squirrelAt(terrain, den, t)`: where it is at clock time t (hopping, running, foraging, alert, flicking its tail, resting), or null while it is up in its tree (night, or outside its share `animals.out` of the bright hours, centred on the sunline's pass). Pure, about 10 µs. How closed the crowns are over a point: `crownsOf(ecology)`; ground an animal may stand on: `open(terrain, θ, s)`. |
| Where are the deer, and what are they doing? | `herdsNear(ecology, theta, s, radius)` (`sim/herds.ts`, in the worker): the herds whose homes lie within a radius, on deer land (`deerLand`: the wild reserve, `herd` and `let-go` districts), from cells two roams a side holding `animals.deer` per km² in herds of about `animals.herd`; each herd's places within `animals.roam` (lying-up spots under closed crowns, grazing in the open), each with room for the herd and a way from home that fords no more than a stream. `deerAt(terrain, herd, i, t)`: deer i at clock time t, in file behind the herd's path or spread round it when settled (grazing, looking up, standing). A herd's day follows the light where it lives (`localNoon`): out to graze as the light comes up, lying up round the peak, out again till after dark; laid out once a day as legs (`prepareHerd`, a few ms; `herdReady`). Pure. |
| How are a day's outings dealt out so nobody is in two places? | `shuffleOf(n, seed)` (`permute.ts`): a seeded permutation with its inverse, never stored. |
| How do I see where everyone is over a day? | The Blueprint tab's "Who is out on the land" (`src/lab/lifeView.ts`): one dot per person over the land plan's maps, with a slider through the day. |
| What happens when the player touches someone? | *Planned:* they become a live agent in `src/sim`, starting from their derived state, and go back to their routine after. |
