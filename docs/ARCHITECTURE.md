# Seam index

Each row is a question you will have while coding, and the one place that answers it. Before writing a helper, look here. A new seam gets its row in the same PR. *Planned* rows name the seam to build when it is needed.

## The habitat blueprint (`src/blueprint`, pure)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| What are the inputs that define a habitat? | `HabitatParams` (`params.ts`). Add a field there, never a constant elsewhere. |
| What follows from them (spin, hull, light, heat, population...)? | `derive(params)` → `Habitat` (`derive.ts`). |
| How strong is gravity, or how dense is the air, at a radius? | `habitat.gravityAt(r)`, `habitat.pressureAt(r)`. |
| How far does a dropped thing drift (Coriolis)? | `habitat.dropOffset(h)`. |
| What shape is the inside (floor, endcap slopes)? | `habitat.profile` (`makeProfile`, `profile.ts`): the meridian curve, sampled by arc length `s`. |
| Where is a point in the world, and back? | `toWorld(profile, {theta, s, h})`, `fromWorld(profile, x, y, z)`, `groundNormal` (`frame.ts`). World axis is +z from the wild wall (z = 0) to the port wall (z = L); spin is +θ. |
| What does the habitat look like from outside (endcap faces, radiator brims, dock spindle, mast)? | `habitat.exterior` (`makeExterior`, `exterior.ts`), sized from the heat in `derive`; shape inputs in `HabitatParams.exterior`. |
| How far is a point from the habitat's outside? | `exteriorDistance(habitat.exterior, p)` (`exterior.ts`): the nearest of `exterior.pieces` (drum, brims, spindle, mast), negative inside. |
| How much of the skin pipes light in, and how much makes power? | `habitat.collectorShare` (`derive.ts`): light collectors sized to the piped light (`pipedLight`, `collectorEfficiency`, `SUNLIGHT_VISIBLE`); the rest of the skin is solar cells (`solarPower`). |
| Is this habitat buildable, and what is odd about it? | `validate(habitat)` → findings `invalid` / `unusual`; `isValid` (`validate.ts`). |
| How do a habitat's people like to shape their land? | Its culture: `CULTURES` / `cultureOf(name)` (`cultures.ts`), named by `HabitatParams.culture` (an unknown name is an invalid habitat): the district styles (`DISTRICT_STYLES`, `StyleSpec` with the care each is at home in), and what each style makes of its land in any climate (`STYLE_LAND`: crown cover, patch grain, wetness, how strongly it holds to them; read by `gen/ecology.ts`). |
| Which habitats do tests run on? | `MATRIX` and `REFERENCE`, `withParams` (`reference.ts`). |

## Generating the habitat (`src/gen`, pure)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| What did the habitat's mind plan for its land (care, gravity bands, valleys, the Gate's faces, the Mountain, river roles, districts and their styles, entrances, landmark sites, the Sunwalk and the ways)? | `planOf(habitat)` (`gen/plan.ts`; made once per habitat by `makePlan`, then kept), the macro layout stage that every later stage reads. Its big shapes, which the land itself is made from, are `landformOf(habitat)` (`Landform`, kept; cheap, no districts): `careAt(s)`, `gravityAt(s)` / `bandAt(s)` (`GRAVITY_BANDS`), `valleyAt(k, s)` / `acrossAt(θ, s)` / `ridgeAt(θ, s)` (the valleys spiral up the endcaps by `plan.spiral`), `faces` / `faceAt(θ, s)`, `mountain`, `rivers` (`PlannedRiver`: role, valley, spring; `routeOf(river, s)`), `endcapAt(wild, g)`, `stairHalfWidth`; the plan adds `districts` / `districtAt(θ, s)` (borders bend with the land) / `borderAt(θ, s)` (the district, how far inside it, the one across the nearest border), `sites` (`Site`: kind, θ, s, size, facing, district; landmarks first, then entrances), `paths` (`sunwalk`, `way`, `trail`). The ring lake is the plan's: `lakeOf(habitat)`, which the terrain uses. Sizes from `HabitatParams.plan`, styles from the culture (`CULTURES` / `cultureOf` in `blueprint/cultures.ts`). Drawn as maps in the Blueprint tab (`lab/planMap.ts`: `floorMap`, `endcapMap`). |
| How high is the ground at (θ, s)? | `makeTerrain(habitat)` (`terrain.ts`): `heightAt`, `standAt` (ground or water surface), `waterDepth(p)`, `lake`; sizes from `HabitatParams.relief`. Noise is sampled at world positions, so it wraps. The floor follows the plan's valleys (`FLOOR_VALLEYS`: it rises from each valley's middle to the ridges between, with fewer hills on the valley floors). Each entrance digs its court (`courtOf(h, site)` / `COURT` in gen/plan.ts): `terrain.courts` (with each floor's height, its depth under the lowest rim) and `terrain.courtAt(θ, s)`; the walls, stairs and floor are the landmarks'. The endcaps are the plan's landforms (`terrain.form`, sizes from `relief.massif`): the Mountain's couloirs, spurs, crags, horn (`HORN`) and cirque (`CIRQUE`) with natural benches; the Gate's faces between buttresses, terraced by kind (`GATE_TERRACES`), the Lift Stair's ramp up the Stair face. |
| How steep is the ground at a point? | `groundSlope(n, θ)` (`gen/terrain.ts`): 0 level … 1 a wall, against true up (toward the axis), from the ground's normal; `slopeAt(ecology, θ, s, ground, step)` (`gen/scatter.ts`) finds the normal from the heights around. Tiles, plants and the meter all use it. |
| Where do the paths run in the land, how wide are they, what are they laid with? | `pathsOf(habitat)` (`gen/paths.ts`, kept): the plan's `paths` (`PlanPath`: kind, `width` from `HabitatParams.plan.pathWidth`; the ways swing gently between entrances) as straight pieces in a grid; `pathAt(θ, s, reach?)` the nearest within its shoulders (or `reach` m of its edge): kind, width, distance from the middle line, the middle point, `layer` (the Sunwalk gravel-pale stone, a way its district's `STYLE_LAND[style].way`, the trail dirt), the piece and how far along. `makeTerrain` levels the ground across each (`gradePath`: `CROWN`, `SHOULDER`; the level taken every `LEVEL_STEP` widths along over the carved land; not in the lake or a river's water); `colorAt` lays the surface over the share of its patch a path covers (`pathCover`, its `cell` argument, so a far path is a faint line), and keeps crowns off the Sunwalk and the ways; scatter keeps plants and stones off. `nearestOnPath` for the debug Go entries. |
| Where do the rivers run, and how deep is the water at a point? | `makeRivers` (`gen/rivers.ts`), laid over the land by `makeTerrain`, which carves their channels and valleys into `heightAt`; `terrain.rivers`, `terrain.riverAt(θ, s)` (in the water or null). A `River` is samples along s (middle θ unwrapped, surface, half width, run, steepness); `riverAt(rivers, θ, s, reach)` the nearest within a reach (banks, valleys), `riverPoint(river, s)` between samples, `nearestSample` / `besideRiver` for the debug Go entries. Each follows its `PlannedRiver` (role, spring, `routeOf`; spiralling up the endcaps, meandering only on the floor), mouths in the ring lake (no lake, no rivers); sizes from `HabitatParams.relief.rivers`. |
| How is the water drawn (the lake, the rivers)? | `buildWater(terrain, tile, origin)` (`gen/water.ts`), called by `buildTile`: the lake's grid and each river's ribbon clipped to the tile, one mesh per tile with `flow` (across, run, speed, steepness) and `dir` (downstream, half width); on tiles too coarse for a channel, the ribbon lies over the ground the tile draws (`WaterTile.drawn`), so a far river never sinks. Shaded by WATER (`materials.ts`): ripples carried by the flow, white water by steepness, the sunline's glint. |
| What grows where, and what colour is the ground? | `makeEcology(terrain)` (`gen/ecology.ts`): `biomesAt(θ, s, h, slope, out)` (shares of the climate's biomes; on the worked floor each district's style leans on them by `STYLE_LAND`, and belts of the closest woods run along the district borders, from `plan.borderAt`), `moistureAt`, `colorAt` (biomes, then gravel by rivers, sand, the lake's and rivers' beds, and rock; with a `canopy` array also the woods' canopy layer: the crowns' colour and height, and with a `wood` array their cover and spacing, from `canopyOf(biome)`), `nearestWood` / `nearestFloor` (debug Go entries: the nearest closed wood, the nearest meadow). With a `floor` array, each point's shares of the `FLOOR_LAYERS` (grass, litter, sand, rock …: 0–255, 8 per point), from each biome's `floor` and the same overlays; tiles carry them as `floorA`/`floorB`. The biomes and where each is planted come from the habitat's climate: `CLIMATES` / `climateOf(name)` in `blueprint/climates.ts`, named by `HabitatParams.climate` (an unknown name is an invalid habitat). Sizes are shares of the radius. |
| What plants are there, and what do they look like? | Each biome's `plants` (`PlantSpec` in `blueprint/climates.ts`: one of `PLANT_FORMS`, height and spacing in metres). `buildTree(form, seed, variant, detail)` (`gen/trees.ts`, ported from Scale): one unit high, wood (bark-atlas uv), leaf cards (leaf-atlas uv, tint, occlusion), crown hull; `LITE_DETAIL` for the lighter tree. Textures: `barkAtlas()` (`gen/foliage.ts`) and `leafAtlas()` (`workers/leaves.ts`, OffscreenCanvas), both drawn in a worker (`kind: 'foliage'`); shapes too (`kind: 'tree'`). |
| Where does each plant stand, and at which level is it drawn? | `gen/scatter.ts`: each plant's own lattice (any cell size gives the same plants). `LEVELS` (full, lite, hull) with their fade bands in sight units (`unit(spec)`); `plantsOf(ecology)` (species with per-level cell sizes and bands in metres), `canopyBand(ecology)` (where the ground's canopy takes over the woods), `cellsNear(ecology, species, level, eye, from?)` (each with its `near` and `far`; `from` 0 for a level that may stand in for a finer one), `plantCell(ecology, plants, key)` (instances per shape, `PLANT_STRIDE` floats each), `cellBytes`. Runs in the generation workers (`kind: 'plants'`). Ground cover (the climate's `cover`, `CoverSpec`: grass on its floor layer) is a species too (`biome` −1, `on` its layer): drawn at the full and lite levels only (`COVER_FADE`, metres), planted with the chance `coverChance(ecology, layer, grid)` (the layer's share on a lattice, blended; `gridOf(species, level)`). Debris (the climate's `debris`, `DebrisSpec`: stones, fallen trunks and branches) the same way but at all three levels, seen by its size (`debrisUnit`), each carrying the ground's slope under it (`PLANT_STRIDE` 7: x, y, z, height, turn, tilt around and along; the kinds shear by it in `plantFrame`). `nearestOf(ecology, plants, species, eye)` for the debug Go entries. |
| What do stones and fallen wood look like? | `buildStone(color, moss, seed, variant)` (`gen/stones.ts`): one lump (ellipsoid, breaks, noise) sampled at three icosphere levels (`icosphere(n)` in `gen/shapes.ts`, the one sphere), vertex colours; the STONE kind adds a grain in metres. `buildFallenWood(form, 'log' or 'branch', seed, variant)` (`gen/trees.ts`): the form's bark on bent tubes, stubs or side limbs, cut ends; drawn with BARK. Workers: `kind: 'stone'`, `kind: 'fallen'`. |
| What does grass look like up close? | `buildTuft(form, color, seed, variant)` (`gen/grass.ts`): a full and a light tuft of blades, one unit high, vertex colours and sway weights; built in a worker (`kind: 'tuft'`), drawn by `PlantView` with the GRASS kind (waves of wind along the axis; the last level sinks tufts into the floor, `userData.sink`). Past 40 m the floor's grass texture is the cover. |
| What hides the sunline from a ground point? | `horizonAt(terrain, θ, s)` (`horizon.ts`): the horizon angles toward the port and the wild end, from the ground in the point's meridian; tiles bake them per point (`TileMesh.horizon`). |
| How is the ground cut into pieces for drawing, and which pieces does a view need? | The tile tree (`tiles.ts`): `makeLayout` (bands along s, columns by circumference), `roots`, `children(layout, k)`, `parent(layout, k)`: a tile halves along s always, around the axis only while its row stays wide enough (`splitsAround`, `splitAround`), so tiles stay about square where the endcaps' rings shrink to the axis. `select(terrain, layout, eye, pixelsPerRadian, maxPixels, fitOf)` by `splitError(layout, k, error)`: the measured error on screen, but never past cells of `FINEST_CELL_PIXELS` (rock walls never get more accurate, so their error alone would split every tile that sees one to the finest level), and cells no bigger than `CELL_PIXELS` (so far tiles carry the woods' edges, rivers and relief), at `ERROR_PIXELS`; a `maxPixels` above it draws everything coarser, each built tile's distance from its own `relief` (`TileFit`): `draw`, `want`, and `keep` (built tiles in use besides those drawn: the ones split to reach them, children waiting for a sibling; the cache must never evict them). `buildTile` (mesh relative to its `origin`, skirts, water (`buildWater`), `morph` towards the parent's surface). |
| What is each panel of the outer skin (solar cell, light collector, starfloor window), how old, how does it look? | `makeSkin(habitat)` (`skin.ts`): `panelAt(skin, i, j)`, `panelOf(skin, p)`, `lookOf`, `isCollector`, `campaignAt`, from integer hashes of the skin's seed; the shader (`render/hullShaders.ts`) mirrors the same hashes bit for bit, so the interior's starfloor rooms read the windows here. `columnsOf` and `skinFlux` give a far habitat's light (its glint). |
| Where are the maintenance drones and their tracks? | `isTrack(skin, i)`, `tracksOf`, `dronesAt(skin, t)` (`skin.ts`): a track in the seam below every `exterior.drones.trackEvery`-th column, each drone back and forth over its own stretch, from seed and clock. `trackMean` is how the far view counts them. |
| What ground does the physics stand on? | `buildPatch(terrain, θ, s, size, cell)` (`patch.ts`): a square trimesh of the ground around a point, relative to its `origin`; built in the worker pool (request kind `'patch'`). It carries the landmarks' walkable solids too (the pavilion's `'solid'` level, near the patch, and the Lift Stair's `buildStairSolid`). |
| What does the skin look like up close (bays, modules, beams, bolts, cables)? | `gen/hullDetail.ts`: each skin panel is a bay of `Skin.modules`² solar modules (`exterior.parts`: module and cell size); `skinGrid`/`skinEye` lay it out for the detail tree (`wantedTiles(skin, eye)`), `buildHullTile(skin, key)` one tile's glass (with its `grid` attribute) and parts, more of them the smaller the tile (`detailOf`, sizes in `PART` as shares of bay, module or track). Each tile owns its parts exactly (`cellsOf` cuts: areas cut, bars by their middle line, fittings by their centre), so any two levels meet. Built in the worker pool as the first part of `detailParts` (request kind `'hull'`, part `'skin'`). `railTop` is where the drones ride. |
| How does detail come in as the eye comes closer (any surface or part)? | `gen/detailTree.ts` (pure): a part lays its cells out as a `Grid` (around, wrapping or not, and along; root and finest levels); `wantedCells(grid, eye)` is the quadtree around a `GridEye` (split within `SPLIT` × a tile's edge, roots within `reachOf(grid)`), `cellsOf(grid, key)` the cells a tile holds with what it owns of each (`CellCut`), `coverOf(wanted, built)` what to draw while tiles stream in (a parent until all its children are built) and whether a part is wholly covered. Every detailed part uses it; never a quadtree of its own. |
| What does the hardware look like up close (spindle plating, berth arms, mast, dishes)? | `gen/hullParts.ts` (pure): `detailParts(habitat, skin)` lists every detailed part with its frame (`'body'` or the despun `'spindle'`), its `Grid` and its `Place`; `partEye`/`partDistance`/`partTiles` place an eye over it, `buildPartTile(habitat, skin, part, key)` builds one tile (the skin's through `buildHullTile`), more parts the smaller the tile (`partDetail`, sizes in `PLATING`, `TRUSS`, `DISH` as shares of the plate, truss width or dish diameter, `exterior.parts.handrail` for what people hold). A truss's or dish's root tile (`wholeTile`) is the whole part and is its far form too. `antennas(habitat)` places the dishes and the mast's whips; `berthArmAt(spindle, i)` (blueprint/exterior.ts) is which way an arm points, for the arms, their collars and the ships berthed there. |
| Where do the landmarks stand, and how far do they keep plants off? | `landmarksOf(terrain)` (`gen/landmarks.ts`), each on its site in the habitat plan (`siteOf(planOf(habitat), kind)`, gen/plan.ts), sized by `HabitatParams.landmarks`: the pavilion on the `lake-pavilion` site (where the Sunwalk crosses the lake), a bridge to each shore along the axis; the summit hut on the `summit-hut` site, hung on the Mountain's cliff (its hall clear of the rock, its anchor and struts buried in it). `frameOf(habitat, landmark)` is its `Place` (z up; the pavilion's x along the axis, the hut's out toward the view; `placeAt(habitat, θ, s, height)` for any spot); `buildLandmark(landmark, level)` builds either kind at a level (`LANDMARK_LEVELS`, gen/pavilion.ts), `sightOf` is how the view picks the level; `keptClear` (scatter skips it), `landmarkTopAt` (what people stand on; the view's `standOn`), `reachOf`. A new landmark takes its plan site here. |
| How does the Lift Stair climb the Gate, and what does a tile of it hold? | `liftStairOf(habitat)` (`gen/liftStair.ts`, kept; null without a ramp wide enough): platforms and legs in plan over `rampOf(habitat)` (z along the axis, w across the ramp, e height above the floor; `world`/`plan` map to and from the world), risers by gravity (`riserAt`), zigzag where straight flights would be gentler than the face. `legShapesOf(stair)` lays each leg out (steps, aprons, the nosing line `lineAt`); `stairTopAt` is its walkable top in plan, `stairStandAt` / `stairKeepsClear` the same over the land (through `landmarkTopAt` / `keptClear`). Its tiles are a `detailTree` grid over the ramp: `stairGridOf`, `stairTilesFor(habitat, eye)`, `buildStairTile` (request kind `'stair'`; far, mid and near detail by tile size), `buildStairSolid` (in every ground patch); on screen `StairView` (`render/liftStair.ts`): the roots are the far form, always kept, finer tiles stream in and replace them only once built. |
| What is the summit hut, and how does it hang on the rock? | `buildHut(hut, level)` (`gen/summitHut.ts`): anchor, corbels, struts, open hall under a slate roof, terrace, launch prow, in the hut's frame (x out from the rock, z up, x = 0 the back wall); sizes from `hutPlan(handrail, scale)` (`hutScale(siteSize, handrail)`: lengths shrink with the site, heights never), `hutTop` its walkable top, `hutBounds` its box. Placed against the rock by `landmarksOf` (gen/landmarks.ts). |
| Where does someone stand on a built thing over (θ, s) where the profile's normal leans far from true up (the Gate, the Mountain)? | `standThrough(habitat, θ, s, from, to, step, under)` (`gen/standing.ts`): up the normal to where the line first leaves the thing through its walkable top. The Lift Stair's `stairStandAt` and the hut's part of `landmarkTopAt` use it. |
| Where is a result kept once it is made for a habitat or terrain (its plan, landmarks, plantings)? | `kept(make)` (`gen/kept.ts`): made on first ask, kept weakly for as long as the object lives. |
| Where are trees planted by hand (the founders' grove; later orchards, avenues)? | `plantingsOf(terrain)` (`gen/planted.ts`): `Planted` plants by species, each where it was planted at its own height; groves on their plan sites (`siteOf(plan, kind)`, gen/plan.ts) of the climate's founders' tree (`Climate.grove`). The scatter stage draws each planted species as a `Species` of kind `planted` (`plantedIn` per cell); `scatterKeepsOff` keeps scattered plants and debris out of a clearing; `plantedCanopy` lays the crowns into the woods' canopy (`colorAt`) so they are there from afar. A new planting is one more maker here. |
| What does the assembly pavilion look like? | `gen/pavilion.ts` (pure): `pavilionPlan(pavilion)` (every size from its radius and the handrail), `buildPavilion(pavilion, level)` for `PAVILION_LEVELS` far, mid, near and the physics' `'solid'`, `pavilionTop(pavilion, x, y)` (its walkable top), `FINISH` (stone, timber, bronze, ceramic for the BUILT kind). |
| How do I put a point into a local frame and back (a hull part, a landmark)? | `Place` {ex, ey, ez, at}, `outOfPlace(place, q)`, `intoPlace(place, v)` (`gen/shapes.ts`). |
| How do I build a mesh as plain arrays (in a worker, or for the renderer)? | `Shape` (`gen/shapes.ts`, pure): `band`, `box`, `block` (any axes), `quad`, `prism`, `sweep`, `append`, `arrays`; `bytesOf(arrays)` for what built arrays hold. A mapped shape (`new Shape(n, colored, true)`) also gives each vertex its place on its surface in metres (`uv`: arc length and height on an `arc`, `planeMap` on flat faces) and the `finish` set when it was added; `mappedArrays()`. The renderer's `ShapeBuilder` (`render/geometry.ts`) is a `Shape` with `build()`. |

## Being in the habitat (`src/world`, pure)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| How thick is the air, and how much haze lies between two points? | `world/air.ts`: `densityFalloff(h)` from the blueprint's `pressureAt`, `hazeAlong(h, emission, extinction, sky, eye, p)` (transmittance and in-scatter, the shaders' reference). |
| How does the camera fly? | `Flyer` (`flyer.ts`): world eye, look and up; up swings toward the axis (rolls over when crossing it), speed grows with height, never below the ground. `walker.land(θ, s, look)` sets down under it. |
| Where are the clouds, and how much of the sky do they cover now? | `world/clouds.ts`: `cloudSpan(h)` (the layer's radii and the stretch of the axis clear of the endcap slopes), `cloudsAt(h, t)` (the hour's cover from a seeded weather drift and afternoon cumulus, and how far the wind has carried them), `coverThreshold(cover)`; sizes from `HabitatParams.clouds` (null: no layer). |
| How much light from the sunline reaches a point? | `irradiance(h, emission, p, n, towardPort, towardWild)` (`sunline.ts`), the reference of the shaders' sum; `sunlineEmission(h, t)` is the brightness along the axis at clock time t (the travelling sun, `HabitatParams.day`), `dayPhase`, `dayLevel`, `emissionMean`, `sunlineNorm(h)` makes the mid floor 1. `lightVector(h, emission, p, port, wild, norm)` is the same light as one direction and strength (for three.js's own lights); `lightShares(...)` splits it into equal shares along the axis (the shadow filter's rays). |
| How does something move in the turning habitat? | `apparentAcceleration(h, p, v)` (`motion.ts`): centrifugal plus Coriolis in the habitat's own frame (Ω = +ω ẑ); `coast` is the RK4 reference the physics is tested against. |
| Which way is up, and how strong is gravity, at a world point? | `upAt(p)` (`motion.ts`): toward the axis (null on it); strength `habitat.gravityAt(r)`. Up never follows the ground's slope. |
| Where is the player, which way is up, where do they look? | `Walker` (`walker.ts`): position in (θ, s, h), heading (0 = +z) and pitch → `view()` eye, look, up (`upAt`); `stride(input)` is the velocity the keys ask of the physics body, `follow(feet)` stands on it; it glides only until the physics has ground. `startOf(profile)` is where a walk begins; the eye stands on a ground function (`terrain.standAt`). |

## Space around the habitats (`src/space`, pure)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| What time is it in the sky? | The sim clock is seconds since `SpaceParams.epochJd`; `jdAt`, `julianDay`, `centuries` (`time.ts`). |
| Where are the Sun, Earth and Moon, and which way does Earth face? | `earthAt`, `moonAt`, `moonFromEarthAt`, `earthAxesAt`, `eclipticAxesAt` (`ephemeris.ts`), J2000 ecliptic, metres. Physical constants of the real bodies live in `constants.ts`. |
| How do I get a position from one frame to another? | `space.frames.position(p, from, to, t)`, `.direction`, `.relative` (`frames.ts`), float64, via the nearest common frame. Ids: `sun`, `earth`, `earth.fixed`, `moon`, `<habitat>.orbit`, `<habitat>.body` (the blueprint frame of `toWorld`), `<habitat>.spindle` (the same without the spin: the dock spindle and its berthed ships). Then `toView` for the GPU. |
| Which habitats exist, and where? | `makeSpace(params).habitats` from `necklace()` (`necklace.ts`): the geostationary ring, home first, neighbours seeded from the world seed. `REFERENCE_SPACE` (`reference.ts`). |
| How do rotations and poses compose? | `Mat3`, `Pose`, `compose`, `invert`, `rotX`, `rotZ` (`pose.ts`). |
| What does the sky hold from a point (Sun, Earth, Moon, eclipses)? | `skyFrom(space, frame, eye, t)` → `SkyView` (`sky.ts`): directions, sizes, body axes, `sunlight` (share of the Sun's disc not hidden); `sunlightFrom(sun, earth, moon)` for any point given the centres relative to it. |
| How does the camera fly outside? | `SpaceFlyer` (`flyer.ts`): float64 eye in one frame, speed a share of the clearance to the nearest body, never closer than `MIN_CLEARANCE`, never beyond `MAX_RANGE`. |
| What does a ship look like? | `shipShape(ShipParams)` → `ShipShape` (`ship.ts`; `torchShip`, `sailShip` by `drive`): parts along the ship's x axis (stern at 0, nose at `length`), a torch's plume length and radius. `deriveShip(seed, berth, drive?)` makes one that fits a berth arm (`SHIP_DESIGN` ranges); the passenger's (the title flight's) is `guestShip(space, habitat)` (`traffic.ts`). |
| Which ships fly where, and when? | `trafficAt(space, habitat, t)` → `ShipNow[]` (`traffic.ts`): each berth arm's visits from the world seed and the clock (`visitIn`, slots of `TrafficParams`), a visit's ship placed by `shipAt` in the spindle frame (torch braking beyond the keep-out, nose first down its corridor on thrusters, pitching over at the stand-off, straight in along the arm at `DOCK_SPEED`, berthed at `berthOf`). The passenger's ship is one more visit, `guestVisit(space, habitat, after, lead)`, on an arm left free for it; pass it to `trafficAt` and the arm's other visits give way. A formula of the clock; promotion to a stepped ship comes with boarding (O7). |
| How is the title flight framed? | `attractLoop(space, start, keepOut, shipLength)` → `Cinema.at(elapsed)` (`cinema.ts`): eye, target, up, fov, the ship's pose and throttle, the title's fade, shot by shot, every distance in outer radii. `chooseStart` picks the moment (habitat in sunlight, Earth gibbous behind it). After Start, `arrival(space, after)` → `Arrival.at(elapsed)`: shots 4 to 6: the passenger's ship (`guest`) to its berth, then their eyes floating down the bore into the spin, each frame with the clock it shows (the clock jumps at cuts, never runs fast). `hubView(habitat)` is where they float out into the Hub (the Inside view's `floatAt`). |
| Where are the stars, how bright, what colour? | `decodeStars(bytes)` (`stars.ts`) on `public/sky/stars.bin` (Hipparcos to magnitude 8, J2000 ecliptic directions, flux relative to the Sun). `calendarOf(jd)` turns a date back into the calendar. |

## Drawing (`src/render`)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| How do I get a renderer? | `createRenderer(canvas, { backends: 'webgpu', depth: 'reversed' })` from `@futuremagic/stage`, once per view (`view.ts`, `outside.ts`). |
| How does a mesh get a material? | A kind from `materials.ts` (`GROUND`, `WATER`, `SUNLINE`, `BALL`, `BARK`, `LEAF`, `HULL`, `allKinds`; own attributes on warm-up stand-ins via `standInAttribute`); the mesh carries exactly the kind's attributes. |
| How does a thing enter the scene? | `stage.add(obj)` (the Stage from `@futuremagic/stage`, made in `view.ts`). |
| How are things lit? | By the sunline as a line light, in the kinds' shaders (no three.js lights): `sunlit(horizon)` in `materials.ts` integrates the emission texture along the axis past the two horizon angles, plus `air.skyLight` scaled by the open sky. `setSunline` loads a habitat's scale and length, `setEmission` the hour's emission profile. |
| How are the clouds drawn, and how do they shade the land? | `render/cloudShaders.ts`: one noise field laid on the cylinder and carried by the wind (`setCloudSpan` per habitat, `setCloudState` per frame); `cloudMarch` ray-marches a crossing of the layer for CLOUDS_NEAR / CLOUDS_FAR (`materials.ts`, both faces of one cylinder at the base radius, made in `view.ts`), `cloudShade` is the same field over a point, applied in `sunlit` to every lit kind. |
| A shader helper several kinds share (a smooth band, …)? | `render/shaderMath.ts` (`band(x, rise0, rise1, fall0, fall1)`; `shows(at)` how much of a pattern at this scale a pixel resolves, `lineAt(x, width)` a soft line at whole numbers, `keepMean(line, share, contrast, r)` joints that keep the mean brightness as they fade). |
| What do built surfaces (landmarks) look like, and how are they lit under a roof? | The BUILT kind (`materials.ts`; attributes `uv` and `finish` from a mapped `Shape`): `builtAlbedo(albedo, st, finish)` (`render/builtShaders.ts`; `courses` for anything laid in courses), lit by `sunlit` through `roofHorizons(p, roof)` (the eaves on either side and the oculus, from the mesh's `userData.roof`; the hut's hall uses it with no oculus). Shared parts of built things: `railing` and `topRail` (gen/pavilion.ts), `Shape.face` for a quad whose normal comes from its corners. |
| How do the landmarks reach the screen? | `LandmarkView` (`render/landmarks.ts`): one mesh per level, the far one always there, the finer ones asked of the pool (request kind `'landmark'`, built by `buildLandmark`) when their finest parts (`sightOf`) grow past half a pixel, freed farther out. |
| How does distance look? | `throughAir` in `materials.ts`: every kind sees through the air, marched per pixel in `HAZE_STEPS` pieces with the sunline's halo integrated exactly; `air` (colour, extinction from the habitat's `air.hazeDistance`, sky light) and `airShape` (`setAir`) uniforms. Reference: `hazeAlong` (`world/air.ts`). |
| How do woods look from afar (past the trees' last level)? | `woodsFromAfar` (`render/groundShaders.ts`), in GROUND: crowns on a jittered lattice of the trees' spacing, shaded gaps, clumps and stands, from each tile point's crown cover and spacing (`horizon.zw`); every scale fades to its mean by the pixel's footprint. Notes, findings and viewpoints: [far-view.md](far-view.md). |
| How does rock look, near and far? | `rockFromAfar` (`render/groundShaders.ts`), in GROUND over the vertex colour before both the near floor textures and the far woods, so near and far keep one colour: where the floor is rock (`floorShare('rock')`), packages of darker, warmer and paler beds dipping across the slope on steep rock, streaks down walls in the habitat's frame, broad iron tones and lichen; bed thickness from `relief.terraceStep` (`setRockBeds`); ground with no rock skips it (`If`). The Gate's faces tint the vertex colour by kind in `colorAt` (`gen/ecology.ts`, from `form.acrossAt`/`faces`). `footprint()` is the one footprint fade for every far pattern. |
| Where does snow lie, near and far? | `gen/snow.ts` is the one rule (`snowFieldOf`, `snowCover`, `tanHolds`, `snowLies`): above the plan's `mountain.snowLine` on the wild end, held on slopes up to friction plus cohesion over gravity. The shader side is `snowOver` (`render/groundShaders.ts`, set by `setSnow`), laid in GROUND over the blended near and far colour. The CPU side is `ecology.snowAt` (bare rock in `colorAt`, so no crowns or grass), scatter's `snowKeepsOff`, and the eyes' `landAlbedo`. |
| How do I look at the interior from high up? | The debug menu's Go entries "Fly: …" (`viewpoint` in `main.ts`, `InteriorView.floatAt(eye, look)`), and the shots views `far-*` (`scripts/shots/views.mjs`). |
| How is something lit that three.js's own materials draw (Actors' bodies)? | The fixed light pool in `lights.ts` (`key`, `sky`, `addLights(scene)`; the only place a three.js light is made; before the warm-up): `shineOn(...)` aims the directional key light from the sunline's `lightVector` at a point, past its horizons, and sets the ambient sky light, both scaled to match the kinds. |
| How do things cast shadows? | The key light's one shadow map (`lights.ts`): ±40 m around the player, its v along the axis, snapped to texels. Its filter follows `SHADOW_RAYS` rays toward the light's equal shares along the axis (`lightShares` in `world/sunline.ts`), so shadows are sharp across the axis and soft along it. Kinds multiply `sunShadow` into their direct light (`sunlit`); Actors' materials get it through the light. A mesh casts or receives with `castShadow`/`receiveShadow` (kinds: `shadow` in the registry); the player's body is on `BODY_LAYER`, which the shadow camera always sees. |
| Where does the camera sit, given the huge distances? | At the origin: meshes are placed at `frame.toView(eye, origin)` (float64), and shaders get the world eye from `view.eye` (`materials.ts`). |
| How does the terrain reach the screen? | `TerrainView` (`render/terrain.ts`): `follow(eye, pixelsPerRadian)` each frame selects tiles, asks the worker pool for missing ones, caches and evicts; sets each tile's `userData.morphFar` so GROUND geomorphs it into its parent's shape before they swap. Its bytes in use (drawn + kept) stay inside `CACHE_BYTES` by `LodBudget` (`tiles.ts`): over 80 % it draws coarser in small steps (a larger `maxPixels`), under 60 % it eases back; the info line says so, and a cache over its bytes (`StreamCache.overBudget`) says OVER BUDGET there and warns in the console. |
| What does the ground look like up close? | `GROUND_LAYERS` / `groundLayer(index, size)` (`gen/groundTex.ts`, ported from Scale's texgen): one tileable albedo + relief (slope, height, occlusion) per floor layer, and its mean colour; drawn in the workers (`kind: 'floor'`), loaded once per page by `loadFloor` into texture arrays (`render/groundShaders.ts`). `floorSurface(color, normal)` blends the point's two largest layers by their relief, mapped biplanar from `setFloorEye` (eye modulo 12 m), and scales them so their mean is the far colour. The texture painting kit (periodic noise, Worley, stamping, `finish`) is `gen/texgen.ts`. |
| How is a texture set up for sampling? | `filtered(texture, {srgb, mips, anisotropy, repeat})` (`render/textures.ts`): linear filters (every sampled texture must be filterable in WebGPU), mips, colour space and wrapping in one place. |
| How does the view stream things the workers build? | `StreamCache` (`render/streamed.ts`): `want([{id, request}])` each frame, most needed first; asks the pool a few at a time, drops its own queued jobs no longer wanted (never another cache's), evicts the least recently drawn (a fresh entry counts as drawn when built) once it holds more than its count or its bytes (`limit`, `maxBytes`); `shed()` for the memory watchdog; `overBudget` when the entries it must keep hold more than `maxBytes`. Terrain tiles, plant cells and the hull's detail tiles use it, all on the one pool every view shares (`sharedPool()`, `workers/pool.ts`). |
| How do plants reach the screen? | `PlantView` (`render/plants.ts`): `follow(eye, dt)` streams each species' cells at each level, shared shapes per form × variant × level, instanced BARK + LEAF meshes (full, lite) or one HULL mesh (hull) per cell and shape (`plantInstances(geometry, data or shared buffer)` in `materials.ts`). Levels hand plants over by `userData.band` (one Vector4 per species and level, dithered per plant by `handOverPick` of its turn), moved by `handover` (below) where a finer level's cell is not built yet; `lodLine()` is its watchdog in the debug menu, `only` draws one level (debug: compare them). The hulls take the leaves' colour (`matchLeaves`: the atlas's `leafMeans` through the cards' `TreeModel.leaf`, over `hullColor`, as `userData.tint`). The atlases arrive through `setFoliage`, the canopy band through `setCanopyBand`. Ground cover the same way, as GRASS meshes. |
| How does one level of detail hand over to the next without a gap? | The rule (Arnd, 2026-10-21): a level is never taken away before the level replacing it is built and drawn; then it hands over (CLAUDE.md). Plants: `handover(bands, pieces)` (`gen/handover.ts`, pure) pulls each boundary in to the nearest unbuilt cell of the finer level, `sweepTo` moves it back out over a moment, `holes(bands, pieces)` counts a level drawing where its piece is missing (must be 0; the last level's count is only the far edge streaming in). Ground tiles: `select` draws a parent until all its children are built (`gen/tiles.ts`). Hull detail: `coverOf` draws the finest built cover (`gen/detailTree.ts`), the far form hides only under a complete one. Landmarks: the finest built level up to the wanted one (`render/landmarks.ts`). A new LOD uses one of these, never a swap by distance alone. |
| How does the player jump somewhere (debug Go entries)? | `InteriorView.goTo(θ, s, heading?)` (`render/view.ts`): drops the body and stands it up again there once the physics has the ground (`Walker.place`). Setting `walker.at` alone is undone by the body on the next step. |
| What is drawn outside? | `OutsideView` (`outside.ts`): the sky dome (`SKY`: Sun, Earth, Moon per pixel), the stars (`STARS`, each quad sized by `starReach` to hold its flare), and every habitat of the ring through `NecklaceView` (`necklace.ts`): meshes when near (`SKIN`, `RADIATOR`, `HARDWARE` from `hullGeometry` in `hull.ts` plus the berth arms, mast and dishes as their parts' root tiles, `SKIN_DETAIL` for the skin up close, each mesh's skin in `userData.skin` via `skinUniforms`), glints through the STARS kind when far. Shader pieces: `skyShaders.ts` (the `outside` uniforms) and `hullShaders.ts`; kinds listed by `outsideKinds()`. |
| How bright is it outside, and how do eyes follow? | `exposureFor(meterable, rays, darkest)` and `adapt` (`exposure.ts`): units where sunlit white paper is 1, anchored to the light in view (`ANCHOR_LEVEL`); the `outside.exposure` uniform scales every outside kind, and `DISPLAY_CAP` (`skyShaders.ts`) keeps exposed light inside the half-float frame. |
| How bright is it inside, and how do eyes follow? | `view.exposure` (`materials.ts`) scales every inside kind (in `throughAir`) and three's lights; `adaptEyes` in `view.ts` meters the crowns over the eye (`crownsClosed` from the canopy raise, `gen/ecology.ts`) into `underCrowns` and `insideExposure` (`exposure.ts`) and follows it with `adapt`, times `insideGlare` (`exposure.ts`): the eyes stop down when the brightest quarter of the frame's rays (`meterRays`, the same grid helper the outside meter uses; `insideRayLuminance` lights the land and adds the air's glow from `hazeAlong`) passes `BRIGHT_VIEW`, as in the glow near the axis. Past the shadow map's reach, GROUND shades the floor under woods by the same `crownsClosed` and `UNDER_CROWNS`. |
| What do the 3D views share (frame loop, held keys, canvas size, right-drag looking, drawing a frame)? | `FrameLoop` (frame loop and the keys held while shown), `moveKeys`, `runFrames`, `fitCanvas`, `dragToLook`, `draw(stage)` (`surface.ts`). Each view is a `PageView` in `main.ts`'s `ViewSlot`. |
| How do I turn arrays into a mesh geometry, and pose it? | `meshGeometry(index, attributes)` (`geometry.ts`); `arraysGeometry(arrays, more)` for a built shape's arrays (position, normal, colour when present, and any `more` attributes its kind lists). Shapes (habitats from outside, ships, drones): `ShapeBuilder` in the same file, a `Shape` (gen/shapes.ts) with `build()`. `setPose(mesh, axes, origin)` places a mesh relative to the eye. `posedMesh(geometry, material, name)` makes a mesh posed by hand; `uploadStatic(stage, mesh)` prepares it on the GPU and drops the CPU copies. |
| How do I give up a mesh for good (a streamed tile or cell, a rebuilt part)? | `freeMesh(mesh, owned)` (`geometry.ts`): out of its parent, its own geometry freed (`'none'` for a shared geometry, a test for the attributes it owns when it shares the rest), and the mesh disposed, which is what makes three's WebGPU renderer drop its render objects. Never `geometry.dispose()` alone (guarded): that leaked every streamed tile. A streamed tile's CPU copies go once the GPU has them: `dropUploaded(mesh, renderer)`, after `stage.prepare` of the mesh while visible. |
| How is something below a pixel drawn as a point of light? | `pointsGeometry(n)` (`geometry.ts`) with the STARS kind: `setPoint(position, color, i, dir, far, flux)` per point (flux relative to full sunlight), `pointsChanged(mesh)` after. Neighbours' glints, drones and ships use it; never a quad array of their own. |
| How are the drones drawn? | `DroneMesh` (`drones.ts`), one per meshed habitat in `NecklaceView`: a HARDWARE mesh near the skin (`moveTo(t)`), beyond `DRONES_WITHIN` drone lengths one STARS point each (`glow`, with `userData.plainExposure` so they take the eyes' exposure, not the stars'). Nothing vanishes with distance (Arnd, 2026-10-21): each far form carries the near one's light. |
| How does the hull up close (skin, plating, arms, mast, dishes) reach the screen? | `HullDetailView` (`render/hullDetail.ts`), one per habitat the eye is near any detailed part of (`NecklaceView.followDetail`): streams every part's `partTiles` through one `StreamCache`, draws each part's `coverOf` in its frame (`Frames`: the body's or the spindle's), each tile a HARDWARE mesh of its parts and, for the skin, a SKIN_DETAIL mesh of its glass; `covers(part)` tells the necklace when a whole part's far form can hide. Below the smallest tiles the shaders carry on (`skinDetailColor`, `hardwareColor` in `hullShaders.ts`): every fine layer fades in by `shows` (its period on screen), lines are box-filtered by `lineAt`, and `keepMean` keeps each layer's mean, so the near view's light is the far view's. |
| How does the free camera stay with a surface to look at it? | `NecklaceView.holdStation(eye, look, t0, t1)`: near a drum the eye turns with the habitat (fully within `HOLD_NEAR` outer radii, not beyond `HOLD_FAR`), and lets go as the eye comes nearer the despun spindle and its parts than the drum (`SPINDLE_NEAR`, `SPINDLE_FAR`); `OutsideView.frame` applies it each frame. |
| How is a ship drawn? | `ShipMeshes(shape)` (`ship.ts`), the one way a ship enters the scene: `shipGeometry` body as HARDWARE (an albedo per part), a torch's plume as FLAME (additive; `userData.flame` sets length and throttle), `pose(axes, stern, throttle)` with `shipAxes(forward, up)`. Far below a few pixels a ship is a point of its own light (`shipFlux`: sunlit hull plus the plume's glow). The title's ship is placed by `OutsideView` (`startAttract`, `stopAttract`), the traffic's by `TrafficView` (`traffic.ts`, one per habitat drawn as meshes, from `NecklaceView`). |
| How do people cross into the spin? | The bore on the axis, in the blueprint as `exterior.spindle.bore` (radius, the Hub's end, the bearing at the port face, the arms' end); drawn by `BoreMeshes` (`render/bore.ts`, from `boreShapes`) in the home habitat's `NecklaceView` meshes: still walls with the spindle, the sleeve turning with the body, BORE walls lit by their LAMP strips (`boreColor`, `BORE_LIGHT`). Inside it the exposure meter sees only its walls (`Meterable.room`, `NecklaceView.insideBore`). |
| What is drawn inside? | `InteriorView` (`view.ts`): terrain tiles, sunline, the player's body, the walker's or the flyer's camera (`toggleFlying`, key F; on foot `zoom(deltaY)`, the mouse wheel, glides from the eyes out to behind the player). |
| How is a person drawn? | An Actors `Figure` (`render/actors/figure.ts`, ported from Dunfinity): `Figure.load(look)`, `update({ feet, up, front, speed, anim }, dt, time)` stands it on the curved floor; `FigureAnimator` (`animator.ts`) plays the pack's clips per move state. The pack: `HUMAN_PACK`, `packFetch`, `humanCatalog()` (`pack.ts`; served and built under /actors/ by the `actorsPack` Vite plugin, fetched by `tools/actors/pack.mjs`). Looks: `playerLook(seed)` (`looks.ts`). |
| What time is it in the habitat? | `InteriorView.time` (clock s since the first dawn), advanced by frame time × `timeScale`; the debug menu's Time group changes it. Nothing reads the wall clock. |
| How does the view run physics? | `InteriorView.simulate(dt, eye)`: asks the pool for a ground patch when the bubble needs one, advances the `PhysicsWorld`, draws each body as a BALL mesh. `dropBall(height)` and `clearBalls()` are in the debug menu's Physics group. |
