# Seam index

Each row is a question you will have while coding, and the one place that answers it. Before writing a helper, look here. A new seam gets its row in the same PR. *Planned* rows name the seam to build when it is needed.

## The habitat blueprint (`src/blueprint`, pure)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| What are the inputs that define a habitat? | `HabitatParams` (`params.ts`). Add a field there, never a constant elsewhere. |
| What follows from them (spin, hull, light, heat, population...)? | `derive(params)` → `Habitat` (`derive.ts`). |
| How thick are the pillars that carry the land over the living layer? | `habitat.pillars` (`derive.ts`): radius, load and gravity of one pillar of the Understorey from `HabitatParams.under` (bay, stone, land), sized against crushing and buckling; `validate` flags pillars too thick for their bay. |
| How strong is gravity, or how dense is the air, at a radius? | `habitat.gravityAt(r)`, `habitat.pressureAt(r)`, `habitat.airDensityAt(r)`. |
| How far does a dropped thing drift (Coriolis)? | `habitat.dropOffset(h)`. |
| What shape is the inside (floor, endcap slopes)? | `habitat.profile` (`makeProfile`, `profile.ts`): the meridian curve, sampled by arc length `s`. |
| Where is a point in the world, and back? | `toWorld(profile, {theta, s, h})`, `fromWorld(profile, x, y, z)`, `groundNormal` (`frame.ts`). World axis is +z from the wild wall (z = 0) to the port wall (z = L); spin is +θ. |
| What does the habitat look like from outside (endcap faces, radiator brims, dock spindle, mast)? | `habitat.exterior` (`makeExterior`, `exterior.ts`), sized from the heat in `derive`; shape inputs in `HabitatParams.exterior`. |
| How far is a point from the habitat's outside? | `exteriorDistance(habitat.exterior, p)` (`exterior.ts`): the nearest of `exterior.pieces` (drum, brims, spindle, mast), negative inside. |
| How much of the skin pipes light in, and how much makes power? | `habitat.collectorShare` (`derive.ts`): light collectors sized to the piped light (`pipedLight`, `collectorEfficiency`, `SUNLIGHT_VISIBLE`); the rest of the skin is solar cells (`solarPower`). |
| How is one blueprint made at another size (a Necklace neighbour, any habitat of the system)? | `scaleBlueprint(base, radius, length, seed)` (`scale.ts`): sizes across (hub, sunline, relief, clouds, landmarks) scale with the radius, the ring lake's width with the length, people at the same density; human sizes and shares stay. Lawful from a radius of about 500 m; `validate` says. |
| Is this habitat buildable, and what is odd about it? | `validate(habitat)` → findings `invalid` / `unusual`; `isValid` (`validate.ts`). |
| How do a habitat's people like to shape their land? | Its culture: `CULTURES` / `cultureOf(name)` (`cultures.ts`), named by `HabitatParams.culture` (an unknown name is an invalid habitat): the district styles (`DISTRICT_STYLES`, `StyleSpec` with the care each is at home in), and what each style makes of its land in any climate (`STYLE_LAND`: crown cover, patch grain, wetness, how strongly it holds to them; read by `gen/ecology.ts`). |
| Which habitats do tests run on? | `MATRIX` and `REFERENCE`, `withParams` (`reference.ts`). One terrain and ecology per habitat for the whole run: `terrainOf(name, params?)` / `ecologyOf` / `flatOf`, and the ring of habitats round one `spaceOf(name)` (`test/worlds.ts`); a test of being the same every time builds its own, on the small habitats where it can. |
| How does the fast suite run TypeScript? | `node --import ./tools/ci/ts-hooks.mjs` (`npm test`): Node's synchronous module hooks resolve imports as the bundler does and strip types with esbuild, without tsx's per-closure `__name` calls (over a second of the budget). Scripts and `test:slow` keep `tsx`. |

## Generating the habitat (`src/gen`, pure)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| What did the habitat's mind plan for its land (care, gravity bands, valleys, the Gate's faces, the Mountain, river roles, districts and their styles, entrances, landmark sites, the Sunwalk and the ways)? | `planOf(habitat)` (`gen/plan.ts`; made once per habitat by `makePlan`, then kept), the macro layout stage that every later stage reads. Its big shapes, which the land itself is made from, are `landformOf(habitat)` (`Landform`, kept; cheap, no districts): `careAt(s)`, `gravityAt(s)` / `bandAt(s)` (`GRAVITY_BANDS`), `valleyAt(k, s)` / `acrossAt(θ, s)` / `ridgeAt(θ, s)` (the valleys spiral up the endcaps by `plan.spiral`), `faces` / `faceAt(θ, s)`, `mountain`, `rivers` (`PlannedRiver`: role, valley, spring; `routeOf(river, s)`), `endcapAt(wild, g)`, `stairHalfWidth`; the plan adds `districts` / `districtAt(θ, s)` (borders bend with the land) / `borderAt(θ, s)` (the district, how far inside it, the one across the nearest border), `sites` (`Site`: kind, θ, s, size, facing, district; landmarks first, then entrances, each over a bay's middle below, `baysOf`), `paths` (`sunwalk`, `way`, `trail`). The ring lake is the plan's: `lakeOf(habitat)`, which the terrain uses; `fromLake(lake, s)` how far from its shore; the lakes on the floor are the `Landform`'s `meres` (next rows); whether a point is in any standing water (or within a margin of it) is `inWater(θ, s, margin)`. Sizes from `HabitatParams.plan`, styles from the culture (`CULTURES` / `cultureOf` in `blueprint/cultures.ts`). Drawn as maps in the Blueprint tab (`lab/planMap.ts`: `floorMap`, `endcapMap`). |
| Where are the big landforms on the floor (the Rings, the Heights), and how high is a point on one? | The plan's `grounds` (`GroundForm`: kind, place, height, reach, facing; on the `Landform`, so `landformOf` and `planOf` both have them), placed from `HabitatParams.relief.landforms` (shares of the radius): Rings round the cylinder across its axis (`ringLine(ring, θ)`, a steep face toward the port, saddles over the valleys, gorges where rivers run), Heights on the ridges between valleys. `landformAt(θ, s)` is the designed surface over the land there (`top`, m; `share` 0 at the foot … 1 at the crest; `face`, how much is a terraced Ring front; `slope`, its rise per metre); shapes in `GROUND`. The terrain raises the land by it (`terrain.ts`: a Ring's front in terraces, `RING_TERRACES`; a Height's flanks in crags and benches, `HEIGHT_ROCK`); `terrain.builtAt(θ, s)` is how high a landform raises the land there, rock and terraces included. The rooms between the Rings step down to the lake (`relief.landforms.fall`): each Ring holds a `step` (`up` is the side away from the lake), `roomAt(θ, s)` is how far a point's room lies below the floor's ends (≤ 0; the plan's `lake.level` is down in the lowest room), `OnGround.room` how much of a Ring's step lies under a point (the Ring stands on the lower room). The terrain lays the land on its room; a river's water rests no lower than its room's level (`rivers.ts`), so it falls where it breaks through a Ring's gorge. They are hollow: the Understorey's deck keeps to the land under them (`levelsOf`, through `terrain.hollowAt`). Entrances keep to a landform's foot (`courtCap`, `COURT_SLOPE`); ways keep to their room between Rings, off a Ring's crest and steep front, and round the Heights (`WAY_CLEAR`), the Sunwalk crosses each Ring at a saddle. The Blueprint land map shades them. Design "Shaped ground". |
| What ground did each district shape in its style, and how high is a point on it? | The plan's `works` (`Work`, `gen/works.ts`: kind, district (−1 in the wild reserve), place, height, footprint radius, facing, `crisp` from the district's care, `vary`), thrown by `makePlan` per district in its style's kinds (`STYLE_WORKS`: stone pyramids, cones and sunken courts; makers' mesas and folds; orchard domes; herd downs and scarps; grove vales; water bowls and stepwells; play half-pipes, amphitheatres and ramps; let-go drumlins and tors, also nature's own in the reserve), clear of courts, the Sunwalk, rivers, the lake, the Heights and the Rings' crests (`WORKS`); the ways are bent round them. Sizes are shares of `relief.landforms.works` radii (`WORK_KINDS`). A work's designed surface is `workAt(work, du, dv)`; over the plan `worksAt(θ, s)` (summed, filed in a grid) and `workOf(θ, s)`. The terrain adds it in `landAt` (`builtAt` includes it); a raised work is hollow (`terrain.hollowAt`), a dug one the deck follows down. The wild's bolder hills up the valleys' sides are `relief.landforms.wild` (`WILD_RELIEF`). Whether a point is inside a district by a margin: `plan.inside(d, θ, s, margin)`. Go → "Fly: the next district work"; shots view `works`. The signature works are works too (`SignatureKind`: the Spiral, the Wave Field, the Tilt; `isSignature`), `relief.landforms.signature` of them, sized as shares of the radius (`SIGNATURE_KINDS`, at most a share of a room between the Rings), placed first by sightlines (`SIGNATURE`: as near the Sunwalk as they fit, a room each where they can, never over a district's seed); the entrances' courts keep off them. Go → "Fly: the next signature work" / "… from the far side"; shots view `signature`. How wide the lake's shore is: `shoreOf(lake)` (courts keep off it). |
| What lies under the land, bay by bay (wellheads, halls, homes, lanes, galleries, cisterns, the Dim)? | `underOf(habitat)` (`gen/under.ts`, kept): the Understorey's grid of bays (`baysOf`) with `useAt(i, j)` (worked out per bay, nothing stored) and `hamletOf`; `baysOfHamlet(under, hamlet, most)` walks a hamlet's bays ring by ring out from its middle (the one way to look round a hamlet); `hamlets` in the plan's order of entrances (the courts residents live under, src/sim), each with its rings in bays; `sharesOf` counts the uses. How high it lies is the terrain's: `levelsOf`. Maps in the Blueprint tab (`src/lab/underMap.ts`, on the panels' shared `MapsPanel` in `src/lab/planView.ts`). |
| How high does the Understorey lie under the land, and where is its floor? | `levelsOf(terrain)` (`gen/underLevels.ts`, kept): the layer follows the land (one height, `layers.living`): at each pillar (`at(i, j)`) its ceiling lies under the lowest ground of the four bays round it by the thinnest soil and the deck, no steeper than `under.grade` toward any lower one near (pillars within a few bays, then coarse samples of blocks further off); `levelAt(θ, s)` runs straight between a bay's pillars (`Levels`: ceiling, springing, floor). `underFloorAt` (`gen/underMesh.ts`) gives the structural floor; `underStandAt` includes a home's raised plinth when placing the player inside it. |
| How is the Understorey built and drawn (pillars, fan vaults, floors, lamps)? | `gen/underMesh.ts`: a `detailTree` grid over its bays (`underGridOf`), tiles wanted only while the eye is below the land (`isBelow`, `underTilesFor`), `buildUnderTile` (request kind `'tiled'`, part `'under'`; far, mid, near by tile size, the same sizes and light) with the lamps' light and the day's down the light wells baked per vertex (`glow`); `underSolid` for the physics' patch below. Through the vault at a bay's middle: under each court its light well (up to the court's glass, `lightWellOf` in gen/courts.ts), and in the next bay its portal faces its lift's open bronze cage (`underLiftOf(terrain, court)`: bay, the way toward the court, side, the height its doors open at above; `underLiftIn(terrain, i, j)` the lift whose cage stands in a bay). On screen a `TiledView` (`underSource`, `inside`: no far form of its own) of the VAULT kind; `follow(eye, want)` asks for the tiles around another point the eye will soon be at (a lift's floor below), and `complete` says whether all it wants are built; `drawnKeys()` the tiles drawn this frame, for what goes with their level (the homes' furniture). |
| What sky does a hamlet show on its ceilings? | `gen/skies.ts` (pure): `SKIES` the catalogue, `hamletSkiesOf(habitat)` each hamlet's pick by its district's style (seeded, kept), `skyOverBay(habitat, i, j)` the sky over a bay (−1: the wellhead, which keeps its stone round the real light, and every bay outside a hamlet). The bay's spandrel carries it as finish `FINISH.screen + k` (gen/underMesh.ts); the VAULT kind draws it with `hamletSky(dir, up, k, day, clock)` (render/hamletSkies.ts: seen infinitely far off, by direction in the eye's frame; `SKY_DRAWN` in the catalogue's order). A new sky is a catalogue entry, its style weights and one branch there. Debug menu `Below: under the next kind of hamlet sky`; shots view `under-skies`. |
| Where do the hamlets' houses stand, and how are they built and drawn? | `housesIn(habitat, i, j, along, around, plinth, levels)` (`gen/homes.ts`, pure, seeded per bay): a hamlet's home bays hold `1 / under.household.bays` plots each (fewer where a bay is too short), each a `House` (plot, body, storeys under the springing, base level with its plot's highest floor, door toward the wellhead, era and build by the district's style, lived or empty). `underHousesIn(terrain, i, j)` (gen/underMesh.ts) gives a bay's houses with its levels. `houseShape(shape, house, levels, handrail, detail)` (`gen/homeMesh.ts`) builds one into its bay's shape at far, mid or near (the same outline, colour and windows at every level); `houseSolid` for the physics. The builds' finishes are `FINISH.brick`, `plaster`, `earth`, `panel` (and stone, timber), drawn by `builtAlbedo` (render/builtShaders.ts, `coursed` for any finish laid in courses); a window's `FINISH.window` glows in the VAULT kind with its vertex colour (the rooms' light). Debug menu `Below: in a street among a hamlet's homes`; shots view `under-homes`. |
| What is inside a home, and how does it change for its inhabitants? | `gen/homeInterior.ts`: `homePlan` derives the clear room, actual doorway, stair and service zone; `homeInteriorShape` builds display-lined walls, floors with stair openings, shallow steps, light coves and the service surface. `homeUseAt(house, clock, period, floor)` gives the household's mode; `homeFittings` gives the same pieces in gathering, focus, rest and stowed forms. `FINISH.domestic` uses a quiet procedural landscape and the shared `screenSurface` anti-aliased panel joints in VAULT. Rooms are built at mid and near only (`RoomDetail`; mid's stair a ramp of its run); far houses are closed, an open door (`Opening.open`) a pane in what is seen through it (`homeDisplay`, the rooms' screens). `houseSolid` gives the physics the house with its open door and the rooms' structure (`'solid'`: floors, walls, stair ramps, no screens). |
| How do furnishings adapt while someone is in the room? | `render/homes.ts` (`HomesView`) furnishes exactly the homes whose rooms the Understorey's drawn tiles build (`furnishedBays(terrain, under.drawnKeys())`, gen/underMesh.ts: mid and near), so furniture comes and goes with its tile in the same frame; two warmed instance pools (`FURNITURE_CAPACITY` pieces each: rounded boxes in near tiles, plain boxes of the same size and colour in mid ones), placed from a fixed origin and repacked only when a piece moves or the homes change; it interpolates their dimensions and leaves a player's occupied floor clear. `PhysicsWorld.setFurniture` updates the same boxes' collisions and removes them on leaving. `app/homeControls.ts` offers resident-led or chosen arrangements inside the room. Intent passes to `PeopleView` through `sim/neighbourhood.atHomeActivity`, changing activities without changing identities. The pool is memory-accounted; layout state sheds on pressure, uploaded geometry drops its CPU copy, and disposal uses `freeMesh`. |
| What hall stands in a hamlet's hall bay, and how is it built and drawn? | `hallAt(habitat, i, j)` (`gen/halls.ts`, pure, kept per hamlet): the kind of hall in a hall bay (`HALL_KINDS`: kitchen, bath, shop, stage, minds, commons), laid out per hamlet nearest the wellhead first (a kitchen, the stage and the minds' room face the square), the shares of kitchens, baths and shops by size and district style; `hallCountOf` counts them. `hallShape(shape, habitat, kind, i, j, along, around, plinth, levels, detail)` (`gen/hallMesh.ts`) builds one into its bay's shape at far, mid or near (same outline; steps, goods and trims nearer); `hallSolid` for the physics. A pool's `FINISH.water` is drawn by `stillWater` (render/builtShaders.ts) in the VAULT kind. Debug menu `Below: at the next kind of hall`; shots view `under-halls`. |
| How is a walled building (walls, windows, doors, roof terrace, garden walls) built at every level of detail? | `gen/building.ts` (pure): a `Block` (body, base, storeys, colour, finish, `Opening`s per side from `sidesOf`) goes through `blockShape(shape, block, levels, handrail, detail)`: plinth, walls, panes proud far, holes mid, set back with sills near, steps down from a door where the floor falls away, the roof's parapet; `wallRun` lays a free-standing wall on the floor; `cornerLevels(rect, levels)` the levels under a rect's corners (stand on the highest floor, stay under the lowest springing); `upright` an upright box. Houses (gen/homeMesh.ts) and halls (gen/hallMesh.ts) are built from these. |
| How does a glass deck show the real land above? | `GlassDeck` (render/glassDeck.ts), stepped by InteriorView after each frame is drawn: while a hamlet with the `glass` sky is over the eye's bay or one beside it (`glassWanted`), it takes six views (one a frame, again every 15 s) from over the land straight above the eye, clear of the tallest crowns, with the frame's own scene and materials (`GlassStage.onLand`: land shown without figures, lit there by `shineOn`, eyes at 1), into a render target like the renderer's frame target (no new shaders), and copies each into an atlas of six faces. `glassSky(dir)` looks it up by world direction for the VAULT kind; the atlas is swapped into its texture nodes, so nothing is rebuilt, and is given up when no deck is near (`dropAtlas`, also on memory pressure). Debug menu `Below: under a glass deck`; shots view `under-glass`. |
| How does one ride a lift between the land and the Understorey? | `gen/lifts.ts` (pure): a lift's doors (`doorsAbove` the court's portal, `doorsBelow` its cage; `liftDoorsNear(terrain, eye, below)` the ones in reach, `doorsHit(doors, eye, dir)` whether a click's ray meets them), the cabin's frame at a share of its ride (`cabinPlace`, `rideOf`), the ride's pace (`rideSeconds`, `rideShare`, `calledFrom`), where one steps out (`outside`), and the cabin itself (`buildCabin`). On screen `CabinView` (render/lift.ts): the one cabin drawn, the last used lift's, and its ride; InteriorView boards, waits for the floor below, rides and steps out. Debug menu: `To a lift: at its doors…`, `Below: at a lift cage…`; shots view `lift-ride`. |
| How do I file things by where they lie on the floor, to find the ones near a point? | `floorGrid(radius, cell)` (`gen/floorGrid.ts`): columns around (wrapping, at least `cell` wide) and rows along, `key(column, row)`, `column(θ)`, `row(s)`; `fileUnder(map, key, item)`. Used by the plan's entrances, the paths' pieces and the places' blocks. |
| Where are the Understorey's bays (its pillar grid), and which bay is a point over? | `baysOf(habitat)` (`gen/bays.ts`, kept): the floor's grid (`floorGrid`) at `under.bay`, rows centred on the flat floor: `around`, `along`, `dTheta`, `bay`, `s0`, `key(i, j)`, `bayAt(θ, s)`, `centreOf(i, j)`, `middleNear(θ, s, sMin?, sMax?)` (a bay's middle near a point: the plan sets every entrance there, so a court's light well comes down where the vault reaches the deck). `baysNear(bays, θ, s, reach)` walks the bays round a point (the one way to look round a point below). `underOf` is built on it (`Under extends Bays`). |
| How high is the ground at (θ, s)? | `makeTerrain(habitat)` (`terrain.ts`): `heightAt`, `standAt` (ground or water surface), `waterDepth(p)`, `lake`; sizes from `HabitatParams.relief`. Noise is sampled at world positions, so it wraps. The floor follows the plan's valleys (`FLOOR_VALLEYS`: it rises from each valley's middle to the ridges between, with fewer hills on the valley floors). Each entrance digs its court (`courtOf(h, site)` / `COURT` in gen/plan.ts): `terrain.courts` (with each floor's height, its depth under the lowest rim) and `terrain.courtAt(θ, s)`; the walls, stairs and floor are the landmarks'. The endcaps are the plan's landforms (`terrain.form`, sizes from `relief.massif`): the Mountain's couloirs, spurs, crags, horn (`HORN`) and cirque (`CIRQUE`) with natural benches; the Gate's faces between buttresses, terraced by kind (`GATE_TERRACES`), the Lift Stair's ramp up the Stair face. |
| How steep is the ground at a point? | `groundSlope(n, θ)` (`gen/terrain.ts`): 0 level … 1 a wall, against true up (toward the axis), from the ground's normal; `slopeAt(ecology, θ, s, ground, step)` (`gen/scatter.ts`) finds the normal from the heights around. Tiles, plants and the meter all use it. |
| Where do the paths run in the land, how wide are they, what are they laid with? | `pathsOf(habitat)` (`gen/paths.ts`, kept): the plan's `paths` (`PlanPath`: kind, `width` from `HabitatParams.plan.pathWidth`; the ways swing gently between entrances) as straight pieces in a grid; `pathAt(θ, s, reach?)` the nearest within its shoulders (or `reach` m of its edge): kind, width, distance from the middle line, the middle point, `layer` (the Sunwalk gravel-pale stone, a way its district's `STYLE_LAND[style].way`, the trail dirt), the piece and how far along. `makeTerrain` levels the ground across each (`gradePath`: `CROWN`, `SHOULDER`; the level taken every `LEVEL_STEP` widths along over the carved land; not in the lake or a river's water); `colorAt` lays the surface over the share of its patch a path covers (`pathCover`, its `cell` argument, so a far path is a faint line), and keeps crowns off the Sunwalk and the ways; scatter keeps plants and stones off. `nearestOnPath` for the debug Go entries. |
| Where do the rivers run, and how deep is the water at a point? | `makeRivers` (`gen/rivers.ts`), laid over the land by `makeTerrain`, which carves their channels and valleys into `heightAt`; `terrain.rivers`, `terrain.riverAt(θ, s)` (in the water or null). A `River` is samples along s (middle θ unwrapped, surface, half width, run, steepness); `riverAt(rivers, θ, s, reach)` the nearest within a reach (banks, valleys), `riverPoint(river, s)` between samples, `sampleS(river, i)` where a sample lies along s, `nearestSample` / `besideRiver` for the debug Go entries. Each follows its `PlannedRiver` (role, spring, `routeOf`; spiralling up the endcaps, meandering only on the floor), mouths in the ring lake (no lake, no rivers); sizes from `HabitatParams.relief.rivers`. |
| Where are the lakes on the floor, and how deep is one at a point? | The `Landform`'s `meres` (`Mere`, `gen/plan.ts`, placed by `meresOf` from `HabitatParams.relief.lakes`, shares of the radius): the great lake first, where the great river leaves the Mountain's foot down the main valley in the reserve (as long as the room before the first Ring allows, the Sunwalk along its shore, the river through it), then the smaller ones in the rooms between the Rings, each the lowest of its throws clear of the ring lake, each other, the Sunwalk, the rivers' lines and the landforms. A lake is a superellipse (`along`, `across`, `heading`; round and bayed when wild, square when made, by `MERE`) at its room's level (`level`, where a river through it rests too). `mereEdge(m, R, θ, s)` how far a point lies from its shore (negative in the water), `mereReach` / `mereShore` / `mereDeep`; over the plan `mereAt(θ, s, reach)` and `inWater(θ, s, margin)`. The terrain digs them last in `landAt` (a shelving bed, the shore brought down to the water and never under it), `standAt` swims on them; courts, works, district seeds, halls, hamlets, orchards and people keep out (the ways are bent round them as round a row of discs). `floorRamp(h)` is the stretch at each end of the floor where the land ramps onto it (lakes keep beyond). Go → "Fly: to the great lake"; shots view `lakes`. |
| How is the water drawn (the lake, the rivers)? | `buildWater(terrain, tile, origin)` (`gen/water.ts`), called by `buildTile`: the lake's grid, each lake on the floor's grid (cells past its shore left out, the points past it drawn in onto it) and each river's ribbon clipped to the tile (not through a lake's water), one mesh per tile with `flow` (across, run, speed, steepness) and `dir` (downstream, half width); on tiles too coarse for a channel, the ribbon lies over the ground the tile draws (`WaterTile.drawn`), so a far river never sinks. Shaded by WATER (`materials.ts`): ripples carried by the flow, white water by steepness, the sunline's glint. |
| What grows where, and what colour is the ground? | `makeEcology(terrain)` (`gen/ecology.ts`): `biomesAt(θ, s, h, slope, out)` (shares of the climate's biomes; on the worked floor each district's style leans on them by `STYLE_LAND`, and belts of the closest woods run along the district borders, from `plan.borderAt`), `moistureAt`, `colorAt` (biomes, then gravel by rivers, sand, the lake's and rivers' beds, and rock; with a `canopy` array also the woods' canopy layer: the crowns' colour and height, and with a `wood` array their cover and spacing, from `canopyOf(biome)`), `nearestWood` / `nearestFloor` (debug Go entries: the nearest closed wood, the nearest meadow). With a `floor` array, each point's shares of the `FLOOR_LAYERS` (grass, litter, sand, rock …: 0–255, 8 per point), from each biome's `floor` and the same overlays; tiles carry them as `floorA`/`floorB`. The biomes and where each is planted come from the habitat's climate: `CLIMATES` / `climateOf(name)` in `blueprint/climates.ts`, named by `HabitatParams.climate` (an unknown name is an invalid habitat). Sizes are shares of the radius. |
| What plants are there, and what do they look like? | Each biome's `plants` (`PlantSpec` in `blueprint/climates.ts`: one of `PLANT_FORMS`, height and spacing in metres). `buildTree(form, seed, variant, detail)` (`gen/trees.ts`, ported from Scale): one unit high, wood (bark-atlas uv), leaf cards (leaf-atlas uv, tint, occlusion), crown hull; `LITE_DETAIL` for the lighter tree. Textures: `barkAtlas()` (`gen/foliage.ts`) and `leafAtlas()` (`workers/leaves.ts`, OffscreenCanvas), both drawn in a worker (`kind: 'foliage'`); shapes too (`kind: 'tree'`). |
| Where does each plant stand, and at which level is it drawn? | `gen/scatter.ts`: each plant's own lattice (any cell size gives the same plants). `LEVELS` (full, lite, hull) with their fade bands in sight units (`unit(spec)`); `plantsOf(ecology)` (species with per-level cell sizes and bands in metres), `canopyBand(ecology)` (where the ground's canopy takes over the woods), `cellsNear(ecology, species, level, eye, from?)` (each with its `near` and `far`; `from` 0 for a level that may stand in for a finer one), `plantCell(ecology, plants, key)` (instances per shape, `PLANT_STRIDE` floats each), `cellBytes`. Where a species' plants stand within bounds, without making a cell: `standsIn(ecology, species, bounds, level, visit)`, the one walk of the lattice (`plantCell` uses it). Runs in the generation workers (`kind: 'plants'`). Ground cover (the climate's `cover`, `CoverSpec`: grass on its floor layer) is a species too (`biome` −1, `on` its layer): drawn at the full and lite levels only (`COVER_FADE`, metres), planted with the chance `coverChance(ecology, layer, grid)` (the layer's share on a lattice, blended; `gridOf(species, level)`). | How does a room sound (its echo, and what is heard in it below)? | `roomAt(terrain, listener)` (`sim/rooms.ts`): a `Room` (T60, first delay, wet share) worked out from the room's real size by Sabine (T60 = 0.161 V / A, with the air's share): an entrance court open to the sky, a bay of the pillar forest (floor, ceiling screen, one pillar), a hall, a home's room; none on the open land. `roomBedsAt` the Understorey's beds (`ROOM_BEDS`: the Dim's room tone, cisterns, works, kitchens, baths, workshops, the minds' halls, a home) from the bays round the listener; `throughDeck` how much of the land comes down a light well; `floorBelow(terrain, θ, s)` the Understorey's floor. `soundscapeAt` carries them (`room`, `through`). |
| What does something near sound like? | `cuesOf(ecology, source, t0, t1, gravity)` (`sim/sources.ts`): the one-shots a `Source` gives (people's steps and what they do, never a voice; deer, squirrels; tenders pruning), on grids and seeded slots of the clock so any cut of time gives the same; `humOf(source)` what lasts while it does it (a drone's role hum, a lift portal, a dancing party's music, a hall stage's, a murmuration's wings and a starling roost's chatter, as loud as the square root of its birds). Each view hands over what it holds near the eye as `Source`s (`PeopleView.heard`, `AnimalView.heard`, `FlierView.heard`, `BirdView.heard`, `liftsNear`, `stagesNear`), in `Hearing.sources`; how far each kind is heard: `HEARD_WITHIN`. |
| When does a piece of music play on the land? | `momentAt(ecology, t, listener, soundscape)` (`sim/moments.ts`): the music moment the listener is in (`MOMENTS`: a summit hut at dawn, walking the Sunwalk by day, the lake at night, going below), each with its piece (`MOMENT_MUSIC`). The player keeps each to once a visit, wants it held a while and leaves long quiet between them (`Sound.moment`). |
| What happens when the player touches someone? | *Planned:* they become a live agent in `src/sim`, starting from their derived state, and go back to their routine after. |

## Sound (`src/audio`)

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| How is sound played? | `Sound` (`audio/sound.ts`, the only `AudioContext`, made in `main.ts`): starts on the first click or key; beds as crossfading segments in two panned chains, calls and cues through HRTF panners at their place, hums as crossfading loops following their source, steps per stride (a push-off on a jump, the rush of air in flight), music streamed through a media element (`sound.music(id \| null)`), a dancing party's or a stage's the same way from where it plays. A room's echo is a convolver with an impulse response made from its `Room` (`impulse`), crossfaded when the room changes; below, the land comes only muffled through a light well; a lift ride has its cabin hum, the ears closing as the pressure changes, each hamlet's chime. The arrival after Start is laid from its score (`sound.arrival(score, elapsed)`), the music moments through the music element. Decoded takes are reported as `sound` to the memory watchdog, kept under `BUDGET`, shed under pressure. The header's switch: `soundButton` (`app/soundButton.ts`); the debug menu's line: `sound.hearingLine()`. |
| How is a sound or a piece of music made? | A line in `scripts/sound/sounds.json` (prompt, kind: bed, loop, call, steps, music; `"model": "clip"` for a short piece), then `node scripts/sound/make.mjs` (scripts/sound/README.md): OpenRouter takes, cut, levelled and encoded into `public/sound` with `index.json`. Never by hand, never in CI. |

## Arrival

| Question | The one way |
|---|---|
| Where does the port arrival leave the player? | `gen/arrivalTerrace.ts` derives the sheltered upper Lift Stair landing and its route from the actual last stair leg: the door the newcomer steps out of (`door`), the walk (`start`, `finish`), the way on (`exit`) and the capsule berth (`berth`: where a capsule waits, the way its door faces, its clear radius; null on a landing too small; `capStopsOf` reads it). `gen/liftStairMesh.ts` builds its canopy, seats, the berth's bronze ring and bronze directions at every detail, with solid geometry for walking. Habitats without a Lift Stair retain their hub arrival. |
| How is the interior revealed after docking? | `world/arrival.ts` (`revealAt`) supplies the timed walk out of the door: the newcomer's eyes, their body's feet, facing and speed (the body walks it), the camera's distance behind them (`chase`, never back through the door) and the widening view; it ends behind the player, and the walking view takes over from the same place. `InteriorView.prepareArrival` waits for the stair tiles; `revealArrival` runs it and hands the exact final pose to the walker. Any key or looking skips to the landing. `app/arrivalCard.ts` introduces the habitat and then shows directions until the player leaves. The transfer from the bore remains a cinematic cut. |
