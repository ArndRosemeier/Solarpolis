## Done

- **v0.001, foundation**: house rules (guards, duplication ratchet, change notes and versioning, CI under 2 minutes, Pages deploy); `src/blueprint` (params → derive → validate, profile, (θ, s, h) frame) tested over the four-habitat `MATRIX`; memory watchdog, debug menu, What's new, Blueprint Lab.
- **Shared render foundation**: `@futuremagic/stage` in AssetGenerator (renderer, Stage, cap, hooks, monitor; from Dunfinity). Solarpolis uses it WebGPU only, with reversed depth.
- **First look inside**: the Inside tab draws the whole interior surface (`src/gen/surface.ts`, a coarse seeded backdrop), the sunline and the haze, from a walker on the floor (`src/world/walker.ts`).
- **Terrain tiles (milestone 2, part 1)**: `relief` in the blueprint (hills, ring lake, terrace step); `src/gen/terrain.ts` height field; `src/gen/tiles.ts` wrapping tile tree with LOD by measured error (reference view ~600 tiles, large ~1100); built in a worker pool; drawn camera-relative; WATER kind for the lake; the walker stands on the ground or the water. Arnd chose built hills and lakes, the deck below follows the ground.
- **Geomorphing**: each tile carries a `morph` offset to its parent's surface; the GROUND kind blends it in over the last 40 % before the parent's distance, so level swaps don't pop (normals still switch; a lighting-only change).
- **Sunline light**: `src/world/sunline.ts` (line-light integral over an emission profile along the axis) and `src/gen/horizon.ts` (two horizon angles per point, baked per tile on a 9×9 grid). The shaders sum the integral in 8 steps per pixel instead of the plan's (r, z) table: horizons need a per-point integral anyway, and it needs no compute pass. The day cycle only rewrites the emission texture.
- **Haze**: `src/world/air.ts` and `throughAir`: density from the blueprint's pressure profile, lit by the sunline (a halo near it), marched per pixel in 10 pieces with the halo term integrated exactly. The plan's froxel volume (compute) is not built: per pixel is exact and needs no compute pass. It becomes worth it when clouds and night lights join the air (milestone 4).
- **Travelling sun**: `HabitatParams.day` and `sunlineEmission(h, t)`: a bright stretch crosses the tube from the wild end to the port end over the daylight hours, the tube dims at night; the sunline mesh, ground light, sky light and haze all follow it. The view's clock runs ×60 by default (a day in 24 minutes); debug menu, Time.
- **Rotating-frame physics (milestone 3, part 1)**: `src/world/motion.ts` (centrifugal plus Coriolis), `src/physics/world.ts` (Rapier with gravity off and the apparent force added per step, a bubble that moves with the player over a ground patch from the worker pool). Debug menu, Physics: a ball dropped from 20 m lands about `habitat.dropOffset(20)` behind the spot below it, tested over the whole `MATRIX`.
- **Character controller**: `src/physics/character.ts`, the player's capsule on Rapier's character controller. Up is toward the axis everywhere (`upAt`), gravity is the spin's, so the view stays upright on the endcap slopes and jumps carry further up them; Coriolis acts in the air; in the lake the body floats and swims. Space jumps. The walker only glides until the first ground patch arrives.
- **The player is an Actors human**: Actors 0.5.0 (packages from its release, the asset pack fetched by `tools/actors/pack.mjs` and copied into the Pages build; the CI check builds without it). `render/actors/` is ported from Dunfinity (pack, figure, animator); the figure stands along the spin's up. Bodies are lit by `render/lights.ts` (a key light from the sunline's `lightVector`, an ambient sky light). The mouse wheel glides the camera from the eyes out to behind the player (Arnd); flying shows no body. Two things belong elsewhere later: the animator should move into Actors (the plan's ask), and the reversed-depth bias fix (`reverseDepthBias` in `figure.ts`: three r186 flips the depth test but not the polygon offset) belongs in `@futuremagic/stage` for every material.
- **Near shadows (milestone 3 done)**: the key light casts one shadow map, ±40 m around the player, turned so its v runs along the axis. Its filter follows 4 rays toward equal shares of the line's light (`lightShares`), 5 steps each up to 6 m: shadows are sharp across the axis and soft along it, sharp at contact and blurring with height. The kinds multiply `sunShadow` into their direct light; Actors' bodies get it through the light. One cascade for now; cascades come when trees and landmarks cast long shadows. The Stage's one-build-per-frame cap needs frames ticking: `InteriorView.build` ticks `stage.frame()` while it loads (a shader compiled after the warm-up used to wait forever). Shaders were validated in real WebGPU (WGSL dump plus `getCompilationInfo`, see SHADERS.md).
- **Biomes (milestone 4, part 1)**: a climate catalogue in the blueprint (`temperate-mild`, the reference; `mediterranean`) and the ecology stage `gen/ecology.ts`: each biome bids for the land by moisture (the lake, hollows, drier up the endcaps), height and slope, with its own patch pattern, sharpened into clear-edged planted patches. It colours the ground tiles now; the plant scattering reads the same shares next.
- **Plants (milestone 4, part 2)**: each biome lists its plants (`PlantSpec`: form, height and spacing in metres, rows for groves). `gen/plants.ts` builds each as a low-poly mesh, three variants, near and far detail; `gen/scatter.ts` plants cells per species (cells grow with how far the plant is seen, 40× its height), one candidate per spacing square kept by the biome share, so every cell comes out the same in any order. Scattering runs in the CPU workers, not the plan's GPU compute pass: deterministic, testable headless, and visible in the WebGL screenshot check; the GPU pass is still the plan for grass blades. The PLANT kind draws instances with sway and a dithered fade at the sight edge. Terrain tiles and plant cells now stream through one cache (`render/streamed.ts`). From the ground in a wood about 2 M triangles are drawn; draw calls (one per cell and variant, about 1000-3000) are the thing to watch on iPads.
- **Fly camera**: `src/world/flyer.ts`, F in the Inside view; E/Space climb, Q/C sink; speed grows with height.

## The outside (plan "Solarpolis: the outside": https://claude.ai/artifact/5aV6F3oGzxDapsjV59haXi)

Arnd approved the whole plan on 2026-10-21: the reference habitat sits in the geostationary ring ("the Necklace"), its axis on the ecliptic pole; radiators are flat brims at the endcaps; the port hub is a despun spindle; ships fly on laser-pushed solar sails or fusion torches (habitats stay solar-only); Earth in 3026 looks like today with smaller, darker cities; the title is a live docking flight that ends in the Hub.

- **Done (O1)**: `src/space`: clock, ephemeris of Sun, Earth and Moon, the float64 frame tree, the Necklace (`makeSpace`), tested against 2026 equinox, solstice and eclipses and over the whole `MATRIX`.
- **Done (O2)**: `derive()` heat is what is released inside (electricity plus the piped share of the sunline light, `pipedLight`); `habitat.exterior` (`src/blueprint/exterior.ts`): endcap faces, radiator brims sized to the heat (stacked inward when one ring per end is not enough; view factor between stacked brims; tip stress checked against `brimMaterial`), despun spindle, mast. The Lab section draws them. Reference: 203 GW, one brim per end out to 5.9 km; long: 5 stacked brims per end.
- **Done (O3)**: the Outside tab (`render/outside.ts`): the home habitat from outside with the real sky at the clock's hour. Sun with limb darkening and glare, Earth (NASA day and night maps, seeded clouds, ocean glint, thin blue limb, dim city lights), the Moon (tidally locked, Lommel-Seeliger), 41,411 Hipparcos stars with their colours, Earth's shadow (eclipse seasons at local midnight near the equinoxes). Eyes adapt to what is in sight; the debug menu's "Night eyes" augmentation opens them for the stars. Data and licences: `public/sky/CREDITS.md`. Star proper motion over 1000 years is not applied yet (the packaged catalogue has none).
- **Done (O4, part 1)**: the skin from outside (`src/gen/skin.ts`): a panel grid (`exterior.panel`), repair campaigns (`exterior.campaign`) and single swaps (`panelLife`) that weather black cells blue, columns of light collectors at `habitat.collectorShare` (new in `derive`: the collectors pipe the `pipedLight`, the rest of the skin makes power, so the reference now makes 92 GW and sheds 130 GW through brims 5.0 km out), and starfloor windows (`exterior.starfloors`) lit from inside. The SKIN shader picks the same panels per pixel from the same integer hashes (checked bit for bit with a compute pass); RADIATOR draws faces and brims, HARDWARE the spindle and mast. The other 23 habitats of the ring are glints (`skinFlux`, flickering as their patchwork turns) until they are 10 px across, then meshes (`render/necklace.ts`). F flies free outside (`space/flyer.ts`): from 50,000 km to a metre off the hull, speed a share of the clearance; 4 looks at the nearest neighbour. Exposure is anchored to the light in view, so dark panels stay dark; the Sun's disc no longer overflows the frame to black.
- **Done (O4, part 2a)**: the title screen. The page now opens on the title (`src/app/title.ts`) over the Outside view's attract loop (`space/cinema.ts`): a torch ship (`space/ship.ts`, drawn by `render/ship.ts` with the new additive FLAME kind) braking at the keep-out distance (`exterior.keepOut`, in outer radii) with Earth behind, then its long lens closing on the habitat, then a run along the drum. `chooseStart` picks a moment with Earth gibbous behind the habitat. The Lab moved to `?view=lab`; the header's name returns to the title. The exposure meter now judges lit surfaces by full sunlight (lightness constancy), and sees the ship.
- **Next (O4, part 2b)**: close-up hardware (panel frames, drone tracks, antennas, berth detail). Then O5 (windows from inside) per the outside plan.
- **Note for windows (O5)**: the outside kinds draw with their own Stage today; a window inside will add `outsideKinds()` to the interior's warm-up and draw the sky through the same uniforms.
- **Later**: more outside material kinds (BRIM, HARDWARE, GLINT, WINDOW); the interior generator places starfloor rooms, the Hub lounge and endcap ports. `toView(eye, p)` stays the eye-relative step; positions from other frames go through `frames.position` first.
